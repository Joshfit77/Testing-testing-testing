Food as the foundation

A table filled with color and purpose.

Whole foods can bring together fiber, vitamins, minerals, healthy fats and naturally occurring plant compounds while adding color, flavor and variety to everyday meals.

01 Color from creation

Berries, rich with color.

Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries and blackberries provide dietary fiber, vitamin C and naturally occurring pigments such as anthocyanins.

  • Fiber
  • Vitamin C
  • Plant compounds
  • Colorful whole food

Blueberry · Strawberry · Raspberry · Blackberry

02 Bright + fresh

Citrus, bright with life.

Lemons, oranges, grapefruit and limes are known for vitamin C, refreshing flavor and naturally occurring citrus compounds.

  • Vitamin C
  • Hydration
  • Natural flavor
  • Whole-food option

Lemon · Orange · Grapefruit · Lime

03 Green + nourishing

Leafy greens, fresh from the earth.

Spinach, kale, arugula, romaine and other greens can contribute folate, vitamin K, carotenoids, minerals and fiber.

  • Vitamin K
  • Folate
  • Fiber
  • Carotenoids

Spinach · Kale · Broccoli

04 From the earth

Roots & spices, warm and grounding.

Ginger, carrots, beets, garlic, turmeric and other roots bring strong flavor, deep color and long culinary traditions to the kitchen.

  • Colorful plant compounds
  • Natural flavor
  • Culinary versatility
  • Traditional use

Ginger · Carrots · Beets · Garlic · Turmeric

The natural cabinet

Traditional remedies for everyday wellness.

Foods and herbs have long histories of traditional use. These ideas are shared for general wellness education and should not replace medical diagnosis or treatment.

See every natural remedy
Digestive support

Ginger

Ginger is commonly used to support digestion and ease occasional nausea.

Try: Steep slices of fresh ginger in hot water with lemon.
Throat comfort

Honey + Lemon

Honey is traditionally used to soothe the throat, while lemon adds brightness and flavor.

Try: Add lemon and honey to warm water or tea.
Digestive comfort

Peppermint

Peppermint tea is commonly enjoyed after meals and may support digestive comfort.

Try: Steep fresh or dried peppermint leaves in hot water.
Calm + rest

Chamomile

Chamomile is traditionally used as a calming evening herb to support relaxation.

Try: Enjoy chamomile tea before bedtime.
General wellness

Turmeric

Turmeric contains curcumin and is commonly valued in eating patterns focused on inflammation support.

Try: Add turmeric to soups, rice, vegetables or warm drinks.
Seasonal wellness

Elderberry

Elderberry has a long history of traditional use during cold and flu season.

Important: Raw elderberries require proper preparation before eating.
Herbal kitchen

Garlic

Garlic has a long culinary and traditional wellness history and provides strong natural flavor.

Try: Add fresh garlic to soups, vegetables, sauces and meats.
Warming spice

Cinnamon

Cinnamon adds natural warmth and flavor to foods and beverages.

Try: Add cinnamon to oatmeal, apples, yogurt or warm drinks.
Aromatic herb

Rosemary

Rosemary is an aromatic culinary herb containing naturally occurring plant compounds.

Try: Add rosemary to roasted vegetables, soups or savory dishes.
Herbal tradition

Thyme

Thyme has a long culinary and traditional herbal history and adds strong flavor to meals.

Try: Use fresh thyme in soups, vegetables or herbal tea.
