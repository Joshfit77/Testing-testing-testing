Narrow Gate Fitness

What you are tapping through are pictures of the real app, captured in demo mode with clearly labelled sample data at iPhone size, joined by tap targets. The exercise demonstrations play live: the app's own animation engine running on your device, not a recording.

  1. Tap Enter on the opening, then Explore with sample data, or Start as a new member to sign up as someone new: five quick questions, the plan they build, their first workout, and that member's own empty app.
  2. Use the five tabs Home, Train, Run, Food, Faith; ‹ goes back; ☰ lists every captured screen.
  3. Open any exercise (Train → Exercises): its demonstration plays live: the app's black-and-gold 2D athlete, with the working muscles lit. Tap the picture to pause, drag to scrub, 1× / 0.5×. All 157 are animated; none has had the owner's sign-off yet.
  4. Typing, timers, GPS, the camera and purchases are not live in pictures; the workout flow is captured step by step.
