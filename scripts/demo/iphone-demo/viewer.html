Narrow Gate Fitness

What you are tapping through are pictures of the real app, captured in demo mode with clearly labelled sample data at iPhone size, joined by tap targets. The 3D anatomical figure is live: a real model running on your device, not a recording.

  1. Tap Enter on the opening, then Explore with sample data.
  2. Use the five tabs Home, Train, Run, Food, Faith; ‹ goes back; ☰ lists every captured screen.
  3. On Train → 3D exercise showcase (or a 3D exercise page) tap the figure: it loads the real 3D player.
  4. Typing, timers, GPS, the camera and purchases are not live in pictures; the workout flow is captured step by step.
