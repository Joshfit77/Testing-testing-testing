      Breathing & bracing

      • Big breath into the belly at the top, brace as if about to be pushed.
      • Hold the brace through the descent and the bottom.
      • Breathe out as you pass the hardest part of the drive or at the top.

      Common mistakes

        Safety

        • Squat inside a rack with the safety pins set just below your bottom position.
        • Use a depth you can control without the lower back rounding.
        • Stop if you feel sharp pain in the knees, hips or back.
