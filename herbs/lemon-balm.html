Lemon Balm

Melissa officinalis · Lamiaceae (mint family) · Calm & Mood, Restful Sleep, Digestion

Benefits at a glance

Lemon balm is a cheerful herb that lifts the mood and calms the nerves. It slows the breakdown of GABA, a calming brain messenger, easing anxiety and restlessness. Combined with valerian, it improves sleep quality. A lemon balm cream speeds the healing of cold sores. It also soothes a nervous stomach and tastes lovely as a hot or iced tea.

Overview

Melissa is Greek for honeybee — bees love its flowers. In the 1600s it was said to 'make the heart merry.' Studies have explored lemon balm for calmness, mood and sleep, often combined with valerian.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Calms anxiety and lifts mood.
  • Sleep: Improves sleep quality, especially with valerian.
  • Skin: Speeds healing of cold sores (topical).

How it works chemically

  • Rosmarinic acid (Polyphenol): Inhibits GABA transaminase, the enzyme that breaks down GABA, so more of this calming messenger stays active.
  • Citral & citronellal (Monoterpene aldehydes): Give the lemon scent; calming and antispasmodic.
  • Caffeic acid derivatives (Polyphenols): Block the herpes virus from attaching to cells (cold sores).

Benefits explained

Calm and mood (Some research)

Rosmarinic acid in lemon balm slows the breakdown of GABA, a calming brain messenger. Trials found improved calmness and reduced anxiety after a single dose and over several weeks.

Sleep quality (Some research)

Combined with valerian, lemon balm improved sleep quality in small studies, including in children with restlessness.

Cold sores (Some research)

A lemon balm cream applied at the first tingle shortened healing time and reduced redness of cold sores in clinical studies.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 300–600 mg (Lemon balm extract), 1–2 times a day.

The herb by itself: 1.5–4.5 g (Dried leaf steeped as tea (per cup)), 2–3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • A soothing tea for nervous tension or restlessness
  • Combining with chamomile for better sleep
  • Easing a nervous stomach

How to prepare

Use fresh leaves generously (a small handful) or 1–2 teaspoons dried per cup; steep covered for 10 minutes. Lovely iced.

Safety & cautions

May increase drowsiness with sedatives. Talk to your doctor before regular use if you have a thyroid condition or take thyroid medicine.
