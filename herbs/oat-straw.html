Oat Straw & Milky Oats

Avena sativa · Poaceae (grass family) · Calm & Mood, Skin & Hair

Benefits at a glance

Oats are a gentle, nourishing herb for the skin and nerves. Colloidal oatmeal contains avenanthramides that calm itching and is a recognized skin protectant for eczema. Green oat extract improved attention and concentration in small studies. Milky oats are a traditional tonic for stress and exhaustion. Oat straw tea is rich in silica and minerals that nourish the body.

Overview

Milky oats (the green oat tops harvested when they release a milky sap) are a traditional nerve tonic. Oat straw is a mineral-rich tea. Colloidal oatmeal is an FDA-recognized skin protectant used in baths for itchy and eczema-prone skin.

What it does in your body

  • Skin: Relieves itching and dryness.
  • Nervous system: Nourishes and calms.
  • Brain: Green oat extract may support attention.

How it works chemically

  • Avenanthramides (Polyphenols (unique to oats)): Block NF-κB and histamine-driven itching — the reason oatmeal baths stop itch.
  • β-Glucans (Soluble fiber): Form a protective, moisturizing film on the skin.
  • Silica & minerals (Nutrients): Nourishing to hair, skin and nerves.

Benefits explained

Itchy, dry skin (Well researched)

Colloidal oatmeal contains avenanthramides, which reduce inflammation and itching. It is an officially recognized skin protectant used for eczema and dry skin.

Mental focus (Some research)

Small trials found green oat extract improved attention and concentration.

Nerve tonic (Traditional use)

Milky oats are a traditional tonic for stress, exhaustion and frazzled nerves.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 800–1600 mg (Green oat (Avena sativa) extract), Once a day.

The herb by itself: 3 g (Dried oat straw steeped as tea (per cup)), Up to 3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • Oatmeal baths to soothe itchy, dry skin
  • A gentle daily tea for frazzled nerves
  • A mineral-rich nourishing infusion

How to prepare

Steep 2 tablespoons dried oat straw in a quart of hot water for several hours. For a bath, blend plain oats to a fine powder and add to warm water.

Safety & cautions

Very safe. Those with celiac disease should choose certified gluten-free products, as oats can be cross-contaminated.
