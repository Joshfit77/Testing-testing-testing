Black Pepper

Piper nigrum · Piperaceae (pepper family) · Culinary, Digestion

Benefits at a glance

Black pepper is the spice that helps the body absorb other nutrients. Its piperine increased the absorption of turmeric's curcumin by up to 2,000% in one study. It stimulates digestive enzymes and stomach acid to help break down food. Piperine also has antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. A few grinds with meals is an easy way to make healthy foods work harder.

Overview

Peppercorns are the dried berries of a climbing vine from southern India. Pepper was so precious in ancient Rome that it was used as currency. Its pungent compound, piperine, can significantly increase the absorption of curcumin from turmeric and of some other nutrients.

What it does in your body

  • Digestive system: Stimulates digestive enzymes and stomach acid.
  • Absorption: Increases how much of other nutrients and medicines the body absorbs.
  • Whole body: Antioxidant.

How it works chemically

  • Piperine (Alkaloid): Inhibits liver and gut enzymes (CYP3A4, glucuronidation) and the P-glycoprotein pump, so compounds like curcumin stay in the body longer.
  • Piperine (heat) (Alkaloid): Activates TRPV1 heat receptors, which stimulates saliva and digestive juices.
  • β-Caryophyllene (Sesquiterpene): Anti-inflammatory via CB2 receptors.

Benefits explained

Better absorption of nutrients (Well researched)

Piperine slows the enzymes that break down certain compounds in the gut and liver. In one study, it increased the absorption of curcumin from turmeric by up to 2,000%.

Digestive stimulation (Some research)

Black pepper stimulates digestive enzymes and stomach acid, helping the body break down food.

Antioxidant (Some research)

Piperine has antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties in laboratory studies.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 5–20 mg (Piperine (black pepper extract), only alongside turmeric/curcumin), With each curcumin dose.

The herb by itself: 0.5–1 g (Ground pepper in food), With meals.

Traditional uses

  • Pairing with turmeric to boost absorption
  • Stimulating appetite and digestion
  • Seasoning almost any savory dish

How to prepare

Grind peppercorns fresh just before using; whole pepper keeps its flavor for years.

Safety & cautions

Safe as a seasoning. Concentrated piperine supplements can change how the body processes certain medicines — ask your pharmacist.
