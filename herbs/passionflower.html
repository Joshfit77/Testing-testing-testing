Passionflower

Passiflora incarnata · Passifloraceae · Calm & Mood, Restful Sleep

Benefits at a glance

Passionflower is a calming herb for a busy, worried mind. Its flavonoids act on calming GABA receptors in the brain. One trial found it eased anxiety about as well as a prescription medicine, with less drowsiness during the day. A cup of passionflower tea at night improved sleep quality in a small study. It also reduced anxiety before surgery in clinical trials.

Overview

Spanish missionaries named passionflower after its intricate bloom, which they saw as a symbol of the Passion of Christ. Native to the southeastern United States, it was used by Indigenous peoples and later in Europe for calming. A few studies suggest it may ease anxious feelings.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Eases anxiety and racing thoughts.
  • Sleep: Improves sleep quality.
  • Muscles: Mild antispasmodic.

How it works chemically

  • Chrysin (Flavone): Binds the benzodiazepine site of GABA-A receptors in lab studies.
  • Vitexin & isovitexin (C-glycosyl flavonoids): Contribute to the calming and antispasmodic effect.
  • Harman alkaloids (trace) (β-Carboline alkaloids): Weakly inhibit MAO; present only in small amounts.

Benefits explained

Calming anxiety (Some research)

Flavonoids such as chrysin interact with GABA receptors. In one trial, passionflower eased generalized anxiety about as well as the medicine oxazepam, with less impact on daily performance.

Sleep quality (Some research)

A small study found that a cup of passionflower tea before bed improved sleep quality.

Pre-surgery nerves (Some research)

Clinical trials found passionflower reduced anxiety before surgery without causing excess sedation.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 400–500 mg (Powdered passionflower herb), 1–3 times a day.

The herb by itself: 1–2 g (Dried herb steeped as tea (per cup)), 2–3 times a day, or once at bedtime.

Traditional uses

  • Quieting racing thoughts before sleep
  • Traditional support for nervous restlessness
  • A beautiful climbing vine for the garden

How to prepare

Steep 1 teaspoon of the dried herb in hot water for 10 minutes; drink an hour before bed.

Safety & cautions

Causes drowsiness and may add to sedatives and alcohol. Avoid during pregnancy, as it may stimulate the uterus.
