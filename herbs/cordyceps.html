Cordyceps

Cordyceps militaris · Cordycipitaceae · Energy & Focus, Men's Health, Immune Support

Benefits at a glance

Cordyceps is a prized mushroom in Chinese medicine for stamina and vitality. In older adults, a trial of the Cs-4 extract improved aerobic exercise capacity. In animal studies its cordycepin raised testosterone by stimulating testosterone-making cells, but human proof is still missing. It also supports immune activity and may help the body use oxygen more efficiently. It is a gentle option for energy, with testosterone benefits that are promising but unproven.

Overview

Wild cordyceps grows from caterpillars high in the Himalayas and was once so rare it cost more than gold. Today supplements come from cultivated Cordyceps militaris or a fermented strain called Cs-4. In Tibetan and Chinese medicine it has long been used for stamina, the lungs and men's vitality.

What it does in your body

  • Energy: Supports stamina and oxygen use.
  • Hormones: Raised testosterone in animal studies.
  • Immune system: Activates immune cells.

How it works chemically

  • Cordycepin (3′-deoxyadenosine) (Nucleoside): Acts on energy-sensing (AMPK) pathways and stimulated testosterone production in Leydig cells in lab studies.
  • Beta-glucans (Polysaccharides): Activate immune cells.
  • Adenosine (Nucleoside): Supports blood flow and cellular energy.

Benefits explained

Stamina and exercise (Some research)

In a 12-week trial in older adults, the Cs-4 extract (1 g three times a day) improved aerobic capacity; results in young athletes are mixed.

Testosterone (animal studies) (Some research)

Cordyceps extracts and cordycepin raised testosterone in animal and cell studies by stimulating testosterone-making Leydig cells. Human trials have not confirmed this.

Immune support (Some research)

Its polysaccharides activate immune cells such as natural killer cells in lab and early human studies.

Traditional vitality tonic (Traditional use)

Used in Tibetan and Chinese medicine for the lungs, kidneys, energy and libido.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 1000–3000 mg (Cordyceps extract (Cs-4 or C. militaris)), Once a day, or split morning and midday.

The herb by itself: 3–6 g (Dried fruiting bodies simmered in soup or tea), Once a day.

Traditional uses

  • Supporting stamina and exercise capacity
  • Traditional tonic for male vitality and libido
  • Supporting lung and immune health

How to prepare

Use cultivated fruiting-body or Cs-4 extracts. Dried cordyceps can also be simmered in soups and broths.

Safety & cautions

Usually well tolerated; may cause mild stomach upset. Stimulates the immune system — avoid with autoimmune disease or immune-suppressing medicines. May slow clotting and lower blood sugar. Avoid in pregnancy and breastfeeding, and stop 2 weeks before surgery.
