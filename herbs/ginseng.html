Asian Ginseng

Panax ginseng · Araliaceae (ginseng family) · Energy & Focus, Men's Health

Benefits at a glance

Asian ginseng is a revered root for energy, stamina and vitality. Its ginsenosides support the body's energy systems and reduced fatigue in clinical trials. Studies suggest it may improve memory, calm and mental performance. It may help lower blood sugar after meals. Several trials found Korean red ginseng improved erectile function in men.

Overview

Panax comes from the Greek for 'all-healing.' Ginseng has been prized in Chinese and Korean medicine for over 2,000 years as a tonic for energy and longevity. Studies suggest it may help with fatigue, mental performance and blood sugar control.

What it does in your body

  • Energy & stress response: Reduces fatigue and supports stamina.
  • Brain: Supports memory and calm focus.
  • Metabolism & circulation: Lowers blood sugar after meals; improves erectile function.

How it works chemically

  • Ginsenoside Rg1 (Triterpene saponin): Mildly stimulating; supports memory and the body's energy systems.
  • Ginsenoside Rb1 (Triterpene saponin): Calming and nerve-protective — ginseng's balancing effect comes from this mix.
  • Ginsenosides (general) (Saponins): Boost nitric oxide in blood vessels and modulate the stress-hormone (HPA) axis.

Benefits explained

Energy and fatigue (Some research)

Ginsenosides influence how the body produces and uses energy. Clinical trials found ginseng reduced fatigue, including in people with chronic illness.

Mental performance (Some research)

Some trials report improved memory, calmness and mental arithmetic after taking ginseng.

Blood sugar and erectile function (Some research)

Ginseng may lower blood sugar after meals, and several trials found Korean red ginseng improved erectile function.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 200–400 mg (Standardized ginseng root extract), Once a day, for up to 3 months.

The herb by itself: 1–2 g (Dried root, sliced and simmered as tea), Once a day.

Traditional uses

  • Supporting energy and stamina during demanding times
  • Traditional support for mental focus
  • A tonic for recovery after illness

How to prepare

Simmer a few thin slices of dried root for 20 minutes, or use standardized extracts. Often taken in cycles of a few weeks on and off.

Safety & cautions

Can cause insomnia, headaches and nervousness. May interact with blood thinners, diabetes and blood pressure medicines, and antidepressants (MAOIs). Avoid in pregnancy.
