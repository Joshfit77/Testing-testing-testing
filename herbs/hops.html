Hops

Humulus lupulus · Cannabaceae (hemp family) · Restful Sleep, Calm & Mood

Benefits at a glance

Hops are best known for flavoring beer, but they are also a gentle sleep aid. Their compounds have a sedative effect, and combined with valerian they improved sleep in several trials. Hops contain a strong plant estrogen that may ease menopausal hot flushes. Their bitter acids stimulate digestion and appetite. A hop pillow is an old tradition for a restful night.

Overview

Hops have flavored and preserved beer since the Middle Ages. Hop pickers were noted to tire easily, which led to their use for sleep. King George III reportedly slept on a hop pillow. Hops are often combined with valerian in sleep formulas.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Promotes sleep.
  • Hormonal system: Plant estrogen that may ease hot flushes.
  • Digestive system: Bitter that stimulates digestion.

How it works chemically

  • 2-Methyl-3-buten-2-ol (Alcohol (from bitter acids)): Forms as hop acids break down; has a sedative effect on the brain.
  • Humulone & lupulone (Bitter acids): Stimulate digestion and have mild sedative properties.
  • 8-Prenylnaringenin (Prenylflavonoid): One of the strongest plant estrogens known.
  • Xanthohumol (Prenylflavonoid): Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory.

Benefits explained

Better sleep (Some research)

Hops contain sedating compounds that act on the brain's sleep-wake system. Combined with valerian, hops improved sleep in several trials.

Menopause symptoms (Some research)

Hops contain 8-prenylnaringenin, a strong plant estrogen. Small trials found hop extracts reduced hot flushes.

Appetite and digestion (Traditional use)

Hops' bitter acids stimulate digestive secretions and appetite.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 300–500 mg (Hops strobile powder (often combined with valerian)), Once, at bedtime.

The herb by itself: 0.5–1 g (Dried strobiles steeped as tea), Once, at bedtime.

Traditional uses

  • Combining with valerian to support sleep
  • Hop-filled pillows for a restful night
  • Easing restlessness and irritability

How to prepare

Steep 1 teaspoon dried hops for 10 minutes. They're very bitter — blend with chamomile or lemon balm.

Safety & cautions

Causes drowsiness; avoid with alcohol or sleep medicines. May not suit those with depression or hormone-sensitive conditions. Hops are very toxic to dogs.
