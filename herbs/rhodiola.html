Rhodiola

Rhodiola rosea · Crassulaceae (stonecrop family) · Energy & Focus, Calm & Mood

Benefits at a glance

Rhodiola is an Arctic root that helps fight fatigue and burnout. Its salidroside and rosavins help regulate the body's response to stress. Studies found it reduced exhaustion in people with burnout. It improved concentration and mental performance during stressful times like night shifts. Some studies also show better endurance and recovery after exercise.

Overview

Rhodiola grows in cold mountain and Arctic regions. Vikings were said to use it for strength, and it has been used in Siberia and Scandinavia for centuries. Studies suggest it may help with fatigue and concentration during stressful periods.

What it does in your body

  • Stress response: Reduces fatigue and burnout.
  • Brain: Improves focus under stress.
  • Muscles: Supports endurance.

How it works chemically

  • Salidroside (Phenylethanoid glycoside): Activates AMPK (an energy sensor in cells), protects nerve cells and helps regulate stress hormones.
  • Rosavins (rosavin, rosin, rosarin) (Cinnamyl alcohol glycosides): Unique to Rhodiola rosea; contribute to the anti-fatigue effect.
  • Tyrosol (Phenol): Antioxidant.

Benefits explained

Reducing fatigue (Some research)

Rhodiola's salidroside and rosavins appear to help regulate the body's stress response. Trials found reduced fatigue and exhaustion in people with burnout.

Focus under pressure (Some research)

Studies found improved concentration and mental performance in stressful situations such as night shifts and exams.

Exercise endurance (Some research)

Some studies show improved endurance and faster recovery after exercise.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 200–600 mg (Rhodiola root extract (3% rosavins, 1% salidroside)), Once a day, in the morning.

The herb by itself: Usually taken as extract. Rhodiola root is rarely used on its own — use the capsule.

Traditional uses

  • Reducing feelings of fatigue during busy times
  • Supporting mental focus and endurance
  • Traditional support for adapting to stress

How to prepare

Take standardized extracts in the morning; it can be stimulating later in the day.

Safety & cautions

Can cause restlessness or trouble sleeping. Avoid with bipolar disorder. May interact with antidepressants, diabetes and blood pressure medicines.
