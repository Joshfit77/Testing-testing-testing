Stinging Nettle

Urtica dioica · Urticaceae (nettle family) · Aches & Joints, Skin & Hair, Culinary, Men's Health

Benefits at a glance

Stinging nettle is one of the most nourishing herbs, rich in iron, calcium and vitamins. It may calm allergic reactions, and a small study found it helped hay fever symptoms. Nettle leaf and fresh nettle have eased arthritis pain in some studies. Nettle root improved urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate in men. It is a mineral-rich tea that supports healthy hair, skin and joints.

Overview

Once cooked or dried, nettles lose their sting and become a mineral-rich food high in iron, calcium and vitamins. Nettle leaf is traditionally used for seasonal allergies and joint comfort, while nettle root is studied for supporting prostate health.

What it does in your body

  • Immune system: Calms allergic reactions.
  • Joints: Reduces arthritis pain.
  • Prostate & urinary (root): Eases urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate.

How it works chemically

  • Quercetin & other flavonoids (Flavonoids): Stabilize mast cells so they release less histamine, and calm inflammation.
  • Lignans & sterols (root) (Plant compounds): Bind sex-hormone-binding globulin (SHBG) and may slow prostate tissue growth.
  • Minerals & silica (Nutrients): Iron, calcium, magnesium and silica nourish the body, hair and skin.

Benefits explained

Seasonal allergies (Some research)

Nettle may reduce the release of histamine and other inflammatory chemicals. A small study found freeze-dried nettle helped hay fever symptoms.

Joint pain (Some research)

In some studies, nettle leaf extract or applying fresh nettle to painful joints reduced arthritis pain.

Prostate health (Some research)

Nettle root extract improved urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate in several clinical trials.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 500–1000 mg (Powdered nettle leaf), 2–3 times a day.

The herb by itself: 2–4 g (Dried leaf steeped as tea (per cup)), 3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • A nourishing daily tea rich in minerals
  • Traditional support during hay fever season
  • Soups, pestos and spanakopita in place of spinach

How to prepare

Wear gloves to harvest. Steep 1–2 teaspoons dried leaf for 10 minutes, or blanch fresh tops for 1 minute to remove the sting.

Safety & cautions

Fresh plants sting. Nettle is mildly diuretic and may affect blood pressure, blood sugar and lithium levels. Avoid during pregnancy unless advised.
