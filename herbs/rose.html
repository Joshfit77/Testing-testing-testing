Rose

Rosa damascena · Rosaceae (rose family) · Skin & Hair, Calm & Mood, Culinary

Benefits at a glance

Rose is a flower of beauty that also calms the heart and mind. Its scent reduced anxiety in small studies and brings a sense of comfort. A trial found rose tea eased period pain and anxiety in teenage girls. Rosewater is a gentle toner that calms redness and refreshes the skin. Rose petals are also rich in antioxidant flavonoids.

Overview

The Damask rose has been grown in the Middle East for centuries for rosewater and rose oil (attar), which takes thousands of petals to make a single drop. Rosewater is a gentle skin toner and a classic flavor in Persian, Indian and Turkish sweets.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Calming and mood-lifting.
  • Reproductive system: Eases period pain.
  • Skin: Tones and calms redness.

How it works chemically

  • 2-Phenylethyl alcohol, citronellol, geraniol (Aromatic alcohols): The rose scent; calming through the sense of smell and mildly antimicrobial.
  • Quercetin & kaempferol glycosides (Flavonoids): Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant.
  • Gallic acid & tannins (Polyphenols): Astringent — tighten and tone skin.

Benefits explained

Easing anxiety (Some research)

Small studies found that rose oil aroma reduced anxiety and promoted relaxation.

Period pain (Some research)

A trial in teenage girls found rose tea reduced menstrual pain and anxiety compared with no treatment.

Skin toning (Traditional use)

Rosewater is mildly astringent and anti-inflammatory, helping calm redness and refresh the skin.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Not commonly sold as capsules — use the petals as tea.

The herb by itself: 1–2 g (Dried petals steeped as tea (per cup)), 1–3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • Rosewater as a refreshing facial mist and toner
  • A soothing, heart-lifting tea
  • Flavoring desserts, lassi and Turkish delight

How to prepare

Steep 1 tablespoon of dried organic petals in hot water for 5–10 minutes. Mist rosewater onto the face after cleansing.

Safety & cautions

Use only roses grown for food or cosmetics, free from pesticides. Rose oil can irritate sensitive skin undiluted.
