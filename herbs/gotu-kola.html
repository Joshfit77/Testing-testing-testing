Gotu Kola

Centella asiatica · Apiaceae (carrot family) · Skin & Hair, Energy & Focus

Benefits at a glance

Gotu kola is a small leaf prized for skin repair and a clear mind. Its asiaticoside and madecassoside stimulate collagen and help wounds, scars and stretch marks heal. Trials found it improved circulation and swelling in the legs. Small studies suggest it may support memory and mood. It is the source of the popular 'cica' ingredient in soothing skin creams.

Overview

Gotu kola is eaten as a salad green in Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia and is used in Ayurveda for memory and longevity. In skin care, its extract (often called 'cica' or centella) is popular for calming and repairing the skin.

What it does in your body

  • Skin: Boosts collagen and wound repair.
  • Veins: Improves circulation in the legs.
  • Brain: May support memory and calm.

How it works chemically

  • Asiaticoside & madecassoside (Triterpene saponins): Stimulate skin cells (fibroblasts) to make type I collagen and speed wound healing.
  • Asiatic & madecassic acid (Triterpene acids): Anti-inflammatory and strengthen vein walls.
  • Flavonoids (Polyphenols): Antioxidant and nerve-protective.

Benefits explained

Skin repair (Some research)

Asiaticoside and madecassoside stimulate collagen production. Gotu kola is used in creams for scars, stretch marks and sensitive skin.

Circulation in the legs (Some research)

Trials found gotu kola extract improved symptoms of poor vein circulation, such as swelling and heaviness in the legs.

Mental clarity (Some research)

Small studies suggest gotu kola may support memory and mood, in line with its use in Ayurveda.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 30–60 mg (Gotu kola extract (TTFCA)), 2 times a day, up to 6 weeks.

The herb by itself: 0.6–2 g (Dried leaf steeped as tea (per cup)), 3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • Cica creams to soothe sensitive, irritated skin
  • Traditional support for focus and calm
  • Fresh in Sri Lankan gotu kola sambol

How to prepare

Toss fresh leaves with coconut, lime and onion, or steep 1 teaspoon dried for 10 minutes.

Safety & cautions

Avoid with liver disease and during pregnancy. May cause drowsiness with sedatives. Not related to kola nut and contains no caffeine.
