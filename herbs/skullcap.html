Skullcap

Scutellaria lateriflora · Lamiaceae (mint family) · Calm & Mood, Restful Sleep

Benefits at a glance

American skullcap is a gentle herb for frazzled nerves. Its baicalin and baicalein act on the brain's calming GABA receptors. A small trial found it improved mood without lowering energy. It has been used for over 200 years in North America for nervous tension and stress. It also helps quiet a busy mind at bedtime.

Overview

American skullcap is named for its small helmet-shaped flowers. It has been used in North American herbalism since the 1700s as a calming tonic. Note that Chinese skullcap (Scutellaria baicalensis) is a different plant used for different purposes.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Calms tension and anxiety.
  • Sleep: Quiets a busy mind.
  • Muscles: Eases tension.

How it works chemically

  • Baicalin & baicalein (Flavones): Bind the benzodiazepine site of GABA-A receptors, increasing calming signals.
  • Wogonin (Flavone): Anti-anxiety effects in animal studies without heavy sedation.
  • Scutellarin (Flavone glycoside): Antioxidant and nerve-protective.

Benefits explained

Calm and mood (Some research)

Skullcap's baicalin and baicalein act on GABA receptors. A small trial found American skullcap improved mood without reducing energy.

Nervous tension (Traditional use)

Skullcap has been a favorite 'nervine' — an herb for frazzled nerves — in North American herbalism for over 200 years.

Sleep (Traditional use)

Used for sleeplessness caused by a busy, worried mind.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 400–500 mg (Powdered skullcap herb (from a reputable supplier)), 1–3 times a day.

The herb by itself: 1–2 g (Dried herb steeped as tea (per cup)), 3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • Easing nervous tension and muscle tightness
  • Supporting calm sleep
  • Traditional support during times of grief or burnout

How to prepare

Steep 1–2 teaspoons dried herb for 10 minutes.

Safety & cautions

Some products have been contaminated with germander, which can damage the liver — buy from reputable suppliers. Causes drowsiness with sedatives. Avoid in pregnancy.
