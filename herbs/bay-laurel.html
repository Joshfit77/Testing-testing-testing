Bay Laurel

Laurus nobilis · Lauraceae (laurel family) · Culinary, Digestion

Benefits at a glance

Bay leaves add depth to food and help with the digestion of heavy meals. They traditionally ease gas and bloating. A small study found ground bay leaves improved blood sugar and cholesterol in type 2 diabetes. Bay leaf oil fights bacteria and fungi in the lab. Whole leaves should always be removed from food before eating.

Overview

Victors in ancient Greek games and Roman generals were crowned with laurel wreaths; the words 'baccalaureate' and 'poet laureate' come from it. Bay leaves add a deep background flavor to soups, stews and sauces.

What it does in your body

  • Digestive system: Aids digestion of heavy meals.
  • Metabolism: May improve blood sugar and cholesterol.
  • Immune system: Antimicrobial.

How it works chemically

  • 1,8-Cineole (Monoterpene oxide): Main aroma; antispasmodic and antimicrobial.
  • Eugenol & linalool (Phenylpropanoid / terpene): Anti-inflammatory and calming.
  • Costunolide (Sesquiterpene lactone): Protects the stomach lining in studies.

Benefits explained

Digestion (Traditional use)

Bay leaves help with the digestion of heavy dishes and are traditionally used for gas and bloating.

Blood sugar and cholesterol (Some research)

A small study found ground bay leaves improved blood sugar and cholesterol in people with type 2 diabetes.

May help fight germs (Traditional use)

Bay leaf oil is antibacterial and antifungal in laboratory studies.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 1000–3000 mg (Ground bay leaf), Once a day.

The herb by itself: 1–2 whole leaves. Whole leaves in cooking — remove before eating.

Traditional uses

  • Adding depth to soups, stews, beans and rice
  • Aiding digestion of heavy, rich dishes
  • Keeping weevils away from flour and grains (traditional)

How to prepare

Add 1–2 whole dried leaves to simmering dishes and always remove before serving.

Safety & cautions

Always remove whole leaves before eating — they stay stiff and sharp and can be a choking hazard. Use only true bay laurel; cherry laurel and mountain laurel are poisonous.
