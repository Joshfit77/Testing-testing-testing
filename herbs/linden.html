Linden Flower

Tilia cordata · Malvaceae (mallow family) · Calm & Mood, Restful Sleep, Breathing & Throat, Heart & Circulation

Benefits at a glance

Linden flower is one of Europe's favorite calming teas. Its flavonoids gently ease nervous tension and irritability. Its tea encourages gentle sweating, making it a traditional comfort during colds and fevers. Soothing mucilage in the flowers calms a scratchy throat. Its honey-like flavor makes it a delightful evening drink.

Overview

Linden (lime or basswood) trees line the boulevards of European cities. Tilleul tea is a beloved French evening drink, and in folk medicine it's used to relax tension and encourage sweating during colds. Bees make excellent honey from its flowers.

What it does in your body

  • Nervous system: Calms tension.
  • Immune system: Promotes sweating during fevers.
  • Throat: Soothing mucilage.

How it works chemically

  • Tiliroside, quercetin & kaempferol (Flavonoids): Anti-inflammatory and mildly anti-anxiety.
  • Mucilage (Polysaccharides): Soothes the throat.
  • Farnesol (Sesquiterpene alcohol): Part of the honey scent; mildly antispasmodic.

Benefits explained

Relaxation (Traditional use)

Linden's flavonoids and volatile oils have made it one of Europe's favorite calming teas for nervous tension and irritability.

Colds and fevers (Traditional use)

Linden encourages gentle sweating and contains soothing mucilage for the throat, making it a traditional cold-season tea.

Calming effects (Some research)

Laboratory studies show linden extracts have mild anti-anxiety effects.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Not commonly sold as capsules — use the flowers as tea.

The herb by itself: 2–4 g (Dried flowers steeped as tea (per cup)), 1–3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • A relaxing evening tea to unwind
  • A warm tea during colds and fevers
  • Traditionally to ease tension-related headaches

How to prepare

Steep 1–2 teaspoons dried flowers and bracts in hot water for 10 minutes.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe. People with heart conditions should check with their doctor before frequent use.
