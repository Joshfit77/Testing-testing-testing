Chasteberry (Vitex)

Vitex agnus-castus · Lamiaceae (mint family) · Women's Health

Benefits at a glance

Chasteberry is one of the best-studied herbs for women's monthly cycles. It acts on the pituitary gland to lower prolactin, a hormone linked to PMS. Several trials found it reduced irritability, mood swings, headaches and breast tenderness. It may help balance the second half of the menstrual cycle. Its effects build gradually over two to three cycles.

Overview

In ancient Greece and medieval monasteries, chasteberry was thought to calm desire — hence its name. Today it is one of the best-studied herbs for premenstrual symptoms such as breast tenderness, irritability and bloating, and is thought to work through the pituitary gland.

What it does in your body

  • Pituitary gland: Lowers prolactin.
  • Reproductive system: Eases PMS and breast tenderness.
  • Menstrual cycle: Helps balance the second half of the cycle.

How it works chemically

  • Rotundifuran & other diterpenes (Diterpenes): Act like dopamine on D2 receptors in the pituitary gland, lowering prolactin release.
  • Agnuside & aucubin (Iridoid glycosides): Anti-inflammatory.
  • Casticin (Flavonoid): Anti-inflammatory and supports the hormonal effect.

Benefits explained

PMS relief (Well researched)

Chasteberry acts on dopamine receptors in the pituitary gland, lowering prolactin. Several trials found it reduced PMS symptoms such as irritability, mood swings, headaches and breast tenderness.

Breast tenderness (Some research)

Trials found it eased cyclical breast pain.

Cycle regularity (Some research)

It may help balance the second half of the menstrual cycle; evidence for fertility is limited.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 20–40 mg (Chasteberry fruit extract), Once a day, in the morning.

The herb by itself: 0.03–0.04 g (Crushed dried berries), Once a day, in the morning.

Traditional uses

  • Supporting comfort during premenstrual days
  • Traditional support for cycle regularity
  • Easing cyclical breast tenderness

How to prepare

Use standardized extracts once daily in the morning. Effects build over 2–3 cycles.

Safety & cautions

Avoid during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and with hormonal birth control, hormone therapy, IVF or medicines affecting dopamine (such as for Parkinson's or certain mental health medicines).
