Horsetail

Equisetum arvense · Equisetaceae · Skin & Hair, Aches & Joints

Benefits at a glance

Horsetail is an ancient plant that is one of the richest sources of silica. The body uses silica to build collagen, supporting hair, skin and nails. A trial found horsetail worked as a diuretic about as well as a common medicine. Early studies suggest silica may support bone density. It should be used only for short periods and in thiaminase-free form.

Overview

Horsetail is a descendant of giant tree-like plants that lived 300 million years ago. It's unusually rich in silica — so much so that it was used to scour pots. It is traditionally used for hair, skin, nails and bone health.

What it does in your body

  • Hair, skin & nails: Supplies silica used to build collagen.
  • Kidneys: Strong diuretic.
  • Bones: May support bone density.

How it works chemically

  • Silicic acid (silica) (Mineral compound): Used by the body to build collagen and connective tissue.
  • Isoquercitrin & other flavonoids (Flavonoids): Increase urine output (diuretic effect).
  • Thiaminase (in untreated herb) (Enzyme): Destroys vitamin B1 — choose thiaminase-free products.

Benefits explained

Hair and nails (Traditional use)

Horsetail is one of the richest plant sources of silica, which the body uses to build collagen. It's traditionally used for brittle hair and nails, but evidence is limited.

Diuretic (Some research)

A clinical trial found horsetail extract increased urine output about as much as the diuretic medicine hydrochlorothiazide.

Bone health (Some research)

Silica may support bone density; early studies are promising but limited.

How much to take (adults 100 lb and over)

Capsule form: 300 mg (Horsetail extract (thiaminase-free)), 3 times a day, up to 6 weeks.

The herb by itself: 2–4 g (Dried herb steeped as tea (per cup)), 2–3 times a day.

Traditional uses

  • Traditional support for stronger hair and nails
  • A mild diuretic tea
  • Bone and connective tissue support

How to prepare

Simmer 1 teaspoon dried herb in water for 10 minutes. Use in short courses only.

Safety & cautions

Contains thiaminase, which breaks down vitamin B1 — use only products labeled thiaminase-free, for short periods. Avoid with kidney or heart conditions, diabetes, pregnancy, and alongside lithium or diuretics.
