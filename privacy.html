Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Last updated: October 2026. This page is a general template — have it reviewed for your own situation and location.

Information stored on your device

Features such as your saved herbs, daily checklist, water tracker and interaction checker choices are saved only in your own web browser (using "local storage"). This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Information you send us

If you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter, we receive the name, email address and message you provide. We use it only to reply to you or to send the newsletter you asked for. We never sell your information. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email.

Services we use

  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security.
  • Photos: herb and fruit photos are loaded from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, which receive standard technical information when your browser requests an image.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Forms: contact and newsletter forms may be processed by a form or email provider (such as Formspree or a newsletter service) under their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links

Some pages may include links to shops. If a link is an affiliate link, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are trustworthy.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please reach us through our contact form.
