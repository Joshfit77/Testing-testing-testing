Blood Orange

Citrus × sinensis (blood varieties) · Rutaceae (citrus family) · In season: Winter

What it does for you

Blood oranges are oranges with striking crimson flesh. Their red color comes from anthocyanins, which ordinary oranges don't contain. They provide plenty of vitamin C and fiber. Their flavor has hints of raspberry. They add stunning color to salads and desserts.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Anthocyanins
  • Fiber
  • Folate

Benefits explained

Immunity (Some research)

One fruit supplies most of a day's vitamin C.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Anthocyanins add antioxidant protection beyond normal oranges.

Weight and metabolism (Some research)

Blood orange extract reduced weight gain in some studies.

How to use it

  • Eat segments fresh.
  • Slice into salads with fennel and olives.
  • Juice.
  • Use in desserts and cocktails.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 medium blood orange (about 130 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose heavy fruit. Refrigerate for up to 3 weeks.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
