Noni

Morinda citrifolia · Rubiaceae (coffee family) · In season: Year-round

What it does for you

Noni is a bumpy tropical fruit from the Pacific known for its strong smell. It has been used in Polynesian traditional medicine for centuries. It is usually sold as juice. Research suggests possible antioxidant effects, but evidence is limited. Its taste is famously unpleasant.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium
  • Polyphenols

Benefits explained

Traditional tonic (Traditional use)

Used in Polynesian medicine for many complaints.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Noni juice showed antioxidant activity in small studies.

Joint comfort (Some research)

A small study found noni reduced arthritis pain.

How to use it

  • Drink small amounts of juice mixed with other juices.
  • Add to smoothies.
  • Use as part of a traditional diet.
  • Avoid large amounts.

How much to eat

One serving: 30 ml juice (about 30 g).

Choosing & storing

Buy pasteurized juice.

Safety & cautions

Noni juice is very high in potassium — avoid with kidney disease. Rare cases of liver injury have been reported.
