Mamey Sapote

Pouteria sapota

What it is

Mamey Sapote is a fruit from the Sapotaceae family (plant family), known botanically as Pouteria sapota. In season: summer.

Mamey sapote is a large tropical fruit with salmon-colored flesh. It tastes like sweet potato, pumpkin and almond. It is rich in vitamin B6, vitamin C, vitamin E and fiber. It is a beloved milkshake fruit in Cuba and Mexico. It is creamy and filling.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin B6
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin E
  • Fiber
  • Potassium

Potential benefits

Energy and mood (Some research)

Rich in vitamin B6, needed for energy and brain messengers.

Skin (Some research)

Vitamins C and E protect skin.

Digestion (Some research)

Very high in fiber.

Best ways to eat it

  • Blend into batidos (milkshakes).
  • Scoop fresh.
  • Make ice cream.
  • Use in desserts.

Typical serving

½ cup (about 100 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy

Possible downsides

  • Generally safe.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
