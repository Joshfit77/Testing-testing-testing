Feijoa

Acca sellowiana · Myrtaceae (myrtle family) · In season: Autumn

What it does for you

Feijoa, also called pineapple guava, is a fragrant green fruit with a sweet, tangy flavor. It is rich in vitamin C and fiber. Its gritty, aromatic flesh tastes like pineapple, mint and guava combined. It contains antioxidant polyphenols with antibacterial activity in lab studies. It's a beloved autumn fruit in New Zealand.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Folate
  • Polyphenols

Benefits explained

Immunity (Some research)

Feijoas are a strong source of vitamin C.

Digestion (Some research)

High fiber supports regularity.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Feijoa extracts show antioxidant and antimicrobial activity in the lab.

How to use it

  • Cut in half and scoop with a spoon.
  • Blend into smoothies.
  • Make chutney or jam.
  • Bake into muffins.

How much to eat

One serving: 2 feijoas (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Ripe feijoas fall from the tree and feel slightly soft. Refrigerate and eat within a few days.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe. Some people find the skin bitter.
