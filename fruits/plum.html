Plum

Prunus domestica · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Plums are juicy stone fruits that come in many colors. They are rich in antioxidants, especially in their skin. Plums provide vitamin C, vitamin K and fiber. Dried plums, or prunes, are famous for relieving constipation. Their sweet-tart flavor is delicious fresh or baked.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin K
  • Fiber
  • Polyphenols
  • Potassium

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Plums contain chlorogenic and neochlorogenic acids that protect cells.

Digestion (Some research)

Fiber and sorbitol support regularity.

Bone health (Some research)

Plums supply vitamin K important for bones.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh.
  • Bake into tarts and crumbles.
  • Roast with meat.
  • Make plum jam.

How much to eat

One serving: 2 plums (about 130 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose plump, fragrant plums that give slightly. Refrigerate ripe plums for a few days.

Safety & cautions

Do not eat the pit kernel.
