Baobab

Adansonia digitata · Malvaceae (mallow family) · In season: Year-round (powder)

What it does for you

Baobab is the fruit of Africa's iconic 'tree of life.' Its pulp dries naturally in the pod into a tangy powder. It is very rich in vitamin C and soluble fiber. It also provides calcium, potassium and magnesium. It adds a citrusy tang to smoothies.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Soluble fiber
  • Calcium
  • Potassium
  • Magnesium

Benefits explained

Gut health (Some research)

Baobab fiber acts as a prebiotic.

Blood sugar (Some research)

Adding baobab to bread lowered its blood sugar response in a study.

Immunity (Some research)

Very high in vitamin C.

How to use it

  • Stir powder into water or smoothies.
  • Add to yogurt or oatmeal.
  • Mix into energy bars.
  • Use in dressings.

How much to eat

One serving: 1–2 tsp powder (about 8 g).

Choosing & storing

Store powder airtight.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
