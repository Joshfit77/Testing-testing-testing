Yuzu

Citrus junos · Rutaceae (citrus family) · In season: Winter

What it does for you

Yuzu is a fragrant Japanese citrus with a flavor between lemon, grapefruit and mandarin. It has more vitamin C than a lemon. Its aromatic peel is prized in Japanese cooking. A yuzu bath on the winter solstice is a Japanese tradition for health. Its scent has been studied for relaxation.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Flavonoids
  • Limonene

Benefits explained

Calm (Some research)

Inhaling yuzu scent reduced stress markers in a small study.

Immunity (Some research)

Rich in vitamin C.

Blood flow (Some research)

Yuzu peel compounds supported blood flow in lab studies.

How to use it

  • Use juice in ponzu sauce.
  • Grate zest over dishes.
  • Make yuzu tea with honey (yuja-cha).
  • Add to dressings.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 tbsp juice (about 15 g).

Choosing & storing

Usually sold as juice or marmalade outside Asia.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
