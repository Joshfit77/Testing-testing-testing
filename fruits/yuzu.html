Yuzu

Citrus junos

What it is

Yuzu is a fruit from the Rutaceae family (citrus family), known botanically as Citrus junos. In season: winter.

Yuzu is a fragrant Japanese citrus with a flavor between lemon, grapefruit and mandarin. It has more vitamin C than a lemon. Its aromatic peel is prized in Japanese cooking. A yuzu bath on the winter solstice is a Japanese tradition for health. Its scent has been studied for relaxation.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Flavonoids
  • Limonene

Potential benefits

Calm (Some research)

Inhaling yuzu scent reduced stress markers in a small study.

Immunity (Some research)

Rich in vitamin C.

Blood flow (Some research)

Yuzu peel compounds supported blood flow in lab studies.

Best ways to eat it

  • Use juice in ponzu sauce.
  • Grate zest over dishes.
  • Make yuzu tea with honey (yuja-cha).
  • Add to dressings.

Typical serving

1 tbsp juice (about 15 g).

When to eat it

Any time of day — with breakfast, as a snack or as a naturally sweet dessert.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone supporting their immune system, especially in cold season

Possible downsides

  • Generally safe.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
