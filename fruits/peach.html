Peach

Prunus persica

What it is

Peach is a fruit from the Rosaceae family (rose family), known botanically as Prunus persica. In season: summer.

Peaches are fuzzy, juicy summer fruits native to China. They provide vitamin C, vitamin A, potassium and fiber. Their skin is rich in antioxidant polyphenols. Peaches are low in calories and hydrating. They are wonderful fresh, grilled or baked.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin A
  • Potassium
  • Fiber
  • Polyphenols

Potential benefits

Antioxidants (Some research)

Peach skin and flesh contain chlorogenic acid and other polyphenols.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Fiber supports regularity.

Skin (Some research)

Vitamins A and C support healthy skin.

Best ways to eat it

  • Eat fresh.
  • Grill halves with honey.
  • Slice into yogurt or salads.
  • Bake into cobblers.

Typical serving

1 medium peach (about 150 g).

When to eat it

With or after meals, or as a between-meal snack.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • People supporting digestion and regularity

Possible downsides

  • Peach pits contain amygdalin — do not eat the kernel. May cross-react with birch pollen allergy.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
