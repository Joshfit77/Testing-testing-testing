Peach

Prunus persica · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Peaches are fuzzy, juicy summer fruits native to China. They provide vitamin C, vitamin A, potassium and fiber. Their skin is rich in antioxidant polyphenols. Peaches are low in calories and hydrating. They are wonderful fresh, grilled or baked.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin A
  • Potassium
  • Fiber
  • Polyphenols

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Peach skin and flesh contain chlorogenic acid and other polyphenols.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Fiber supports regularity.

Skin (Some research)

Vitamins A and C support healthy skin.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh.
  • Grill halves with honey.
  • Slice into yogurt or salads.
  • Bake into cobblers.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 medium peach (about 150 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose fragrant peaches that give slightly. Ripen on the counter, then refrigerate.

Safety & cautions

Peach pits contain amygdalin — do not eat the kernel. May cross-react with birch pollen allergy.
