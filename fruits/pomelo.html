Pomelo

Citrus maxima

What it is

Pomelo is a fruit from the Rutaceae family (citrus family), known botanically as Citrus maxima. In season: winter.

Pomelo is the largest citrus fruit, an ancestor of the grapefruit. It has a mild, sweet flavor. One pomelo provides several days' worth of vitamin C. It is rich in fiber and potassium. It's a symbol of prosperity at Lunar New Year.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Potassium

Potential benefits

Immunity (Some research)

Extremely rich in vitamin C.

Digestion (Some research)

Provides fiber for regularity.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains naringenin.

Best ways to eat it

  • Peel and eat segments.
  • Toss into Thai pomelo salad.
  • Add to seafood dishes.
  • Candy the peel.

Typical serving

1 cup segments (about 190 g).

When to eat it

With or after meals, or as a between-meal snack.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone supporting their immune system, especially in cold season
  • People supporting digestion and regularity

Possible downsides

  • Like grapefruit, pomelo can interact with many medicines — ask your pharmacist.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
