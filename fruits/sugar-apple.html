Sugar Apple (Sweetsop)

Annona squamosa · Annonaceae (custard apple family) · In season: Summer to autumn

What it does for you

Sugar apple is a sweet tropical fruit with knobby green segments. Its creamy flesh tastes like custard. It provides vitamin C, vitamin B6 and fiber. It's rich in natural sugars for energy. It is a favorite in India and the Caribbean.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin B6
  • Fiber
  • Magnesium

Benefits explained

Energy (Traditional use)

Rich in natural sugars.

Immunity (Some research)

Good source of vitamin C.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains polyphenols.

How to use it

  • Pull apart segments and eat the flesh.
  • Blend into milkshakes.
  • Make ice cream.
  • Eat chilled.

How much to eat

One serving: ½ fruit (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Ripe when segments separate slightly.

Safety & cautions

Seeds are toxic — do not eat or crush them.
