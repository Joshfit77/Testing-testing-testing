Sugar Apple (Sweetsop)

Annona squamosa

What it is

Sugar Apple (Sweetsop) is a fruit from the Annonaceae family (custard apple family), known botanically as Annona squamosa. In season: summer to autumn.

Sugar apple is a sweet tropical fruit with knobby green segments. Its creamy flesh tastes like custard. It provides vitamin C, vitamin B6 and fiber. It's rich in natural sugars for energy. It is a favorite in India and the Caribbean.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin B6
  • Fiber
  • Magnesium

Potential benefits

Energy (Traditional use)

Rich in natural sugars.

Immunity (Some research)

Good source of vitamin C.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains polyphenols.

Best ways to eat it

  • Pull apart segments and eat the flesh.
  • Blend into milkshakes.
  • Make ice cream.
  • Eat chilled.

Typical serving

½ fruit (about 100 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy
  • Anyone supporting their immune system, especially in cold season

Possible downsides

  • Seeds are toxic — do not eat or crush them.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
