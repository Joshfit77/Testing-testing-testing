Honeydew

Cucumis melo (Inodorus group)

What it is

Honeydew is a fruit from the Cucurbitaceae family (gourd family), known botanically as Cucumis melo (Inodorus group). In season: summer.

Honeydew is a sweet, pale green melon that is about 90% water. It hydrates and provides potassium for healthy blood pressure. It is a good source of vitamin C for immunity and skin. Low in calories, it makes a refreshing snack. Its mild sweetness pairs beautifully with mint and lime.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium
  • Vitamin B6
  • Water

Potential benefits

Hydration (Traditional use)

Its high water and electrolyte content helps hydration.

Blood pressure (Some research)

Potassium supports healthy blood pressure.

Skin (Traditional use)

Vitamin C supports collagen production.

Best ways to eat it

  • Eat chilled cubes.
  • Blend into agua fresca.
  • Toss with mint, lime and feta.
  • Add to fruit salads.

Typical serving

1 cup cubes (about 170 g).

When to eat it

Any time of day — with breakfast, as a snack or as a naturally sweet dessert.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • People supporting heart health and blood pressure

Possible downsides

  • Wash the rind before cutting. Generally safe.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
