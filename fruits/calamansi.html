Calamansi

Citrus × microcarpa · Rutaceae (citrus family) · In season: Year-round

What it does for you

Calamansi is a tiny Filipino citrus that tastes like a lime crossed with a mandarin. It is rich in vitamin C. Calamansi juice with honey is a traditional cough remedy in the Philippines. It brightens marinades and dipping sauces. It makes a refreshing juice.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Flavonoids
  • Citric acid

Benefits explained

Coughs (Traditional use)

Calamansi juice with honey is a traditional cough and sore throat remedy.

Immunity (Some research)

Rich in vitamin C.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains flavonoids.

How to use it

  • Squeeze into water with honey.
  • Add to soy sauce for dipping.
  • Use in marinades.
  • Make calamansi juice.

How much to eat

One serving: Juice of 3 calamansi (about 15 g).

Choosing & storing

Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

Safety & cautions

Acid may erode tooth enamel.
