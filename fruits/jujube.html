Jujube (Red Date)

Ziziphus jujuba

What it is

Jujube (Red Date) is a fruit from the Rhamnaceae family (buckthorn family), known botanically as Ziziphus jujuba. In season: autumn.

Jujubes, or Chinese red dates, have been used in Chinese medicine for thousands of years. They are traditionally used to calm the mind and improve sleep. They are rich in vitamin C, fiber and saponins. Dried jujubes taste like dates. They are often simmered in teas and soups.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Potassium
  • Saponins

Potential benefits

Sleep and calm (Some research)

Jujube saponins acted on calming GABA pathways in laboratory studies.

Constipation (Some research)

Jujube extract improved chronic constipation in a small trial.

Energy (Traditional use)

A traditional tonic for tiredness.

Best ways to eat it

  • Simmer dried jujubes in tea.
  • Add to soups and congee.
  • Eat fresh like an apple.
  • Snack on dried jujubes.

Typical serving

5 dried jujubes (about 30 g).

When to eat it

An evening snack about an hour before bed may suit it best.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • People working on restful sleep
  • People supporting digestion and regularity

Possible downsides

  • Jujube may increase drowsiness with sleep medicines.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
