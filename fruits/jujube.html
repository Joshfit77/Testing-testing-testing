Jujube (Red Date)

Ziziphus jujuba · Rhamnaceae (buckthorn family) · In season: Autumn

What it does for you

Jujubes, or Chinese red dates, have been used in Chinese medicine for thousands of years. They are traditionally used to calm the mind and improve sleep. They are rich in vitamin C, fiber and saponins. Dried jujubes taste like dates. They are often simmered in teas and soups.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Potassium
  • Saponins

Benefits explained

Sleep and calm (Some research)

Jujube saponins acted on calming GABA pathways in laboratory studies.

Constipation (Some research)

Jujube extract improved chronic constipation in a small trial.

Energy (Traditional use)

A traditional tonic for tiredness.

How to use it

  • Simmer dried jujubes in tea.
  • Add to soups and congee.
  • Eat fresh like an apple.
  • Snack on dried jujubes.

How much to eat

One serving: 5 dried jujubes (about 30 g).

Choosing & storing

Fresh jujubes look like small apples; dried ones keep for months.

Safety & cautions

Jujube may increase drowsiness with sleep medicines.
