Loquat

Eriobotrya japonica · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Spring

What it does for you

Loquats are small orange fruits with a sweet, apricot-like flavor. They are rich in vitamin A and provide fiber and potassium. Loquat leaf syrup is a traditional Chinese cough remedy. They are among the first fruits of spring. Enjoy them fresh or in jams.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin A
  • Fiber
  • Potassium
  • Manganese

Benefits explained

Coughs (Traditional use)

Loquat leaf syrup (pipa gao) is a traditional cough remedy in Chinese medicine.

Eye and skin health (Some research)

Rich in beta-carotene.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Provides fiber.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh, peeled.
  • Make jam or chutney.
  • Add to fruit salads.
  • Poach.

How much to eat

One serving: 5 loquats (about 50 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose orange, slightly soft fruit. Eat within a few days.

Safety & cautions

Never eat the seeds — they contain cyanide-forming compounds.
