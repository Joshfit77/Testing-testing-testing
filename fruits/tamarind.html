Tamarind

Tamarindus indica · Fabaceae (pea family) · In season: Winter to spring

What it does for you

Tamarind is a pod fruit with tangy-sweet sticky pulp. It is used in cooking from India to Mexico. It provides magnesium, potassium and polyphenols. It is a traditional natural laxative. Its tart flavor balances curries, chutneys and drinks.

Key nutrients

  • Magnesium
  • Potassium
  • Iron
  • Fiber
  • Tartaric acid

Benefits explained

Digestion (Traditional use)

Traditionally used as a gentle laxative.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Rich in polyphenols.

Cooling (Traditional use)

Tamarind drinks are traditionally refreshing in heat.

How to use it

  • Use paste in pad thai and curries.
  • Make agua de tamarindo.
  • Blend into chutneys.
  • Add to marinades.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 tbsp paste (about 20 g).

Choosing & storing

Store pulp blocks in the fridge.

Safety & cautions

High in natural sugar. May slightly increase absorption of aspirin.
