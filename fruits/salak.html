Salak (Snake Fruit)

Salacca zalacca · Arecaceae (palm family) · In season: Year-round

What it does for you

Salak, or snake fruit, has scaly reddish-brown skin like a snake. Inside are sweet, crunchy, apple-like lobes. It provides fiber, potassium and antioxidants. It is popular in Indonesia and Malaysia. It has a sweet-tart, slightly astringent flavor.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Potassium
  • Vitamin C
  • Polyphenols

Benefits explained

Digestion (Traditional use)

Traditionally eaten to help with loose stools.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains chlorogenic acid and other polyphenols.

Energy (Traditional use)

Provides natural sugars.

How to use it

  • Peel the scaly skin and eat the lobes.
  • Add to fruit salads.
  • Make pickles.
  • Eat chilled.

How much to eat

One serving: 3 fruits (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Store at room temperature for a few days.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe; eating many unripe fruits can cause constipation.
