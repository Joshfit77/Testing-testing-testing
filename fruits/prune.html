Prune (Dried Plum)

Prunus domestica · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Year-round (dried)

What it does for you

Prunes are dried plums famous for relieving constipation. They contain fiber, sorbitol and polyphenols that soften stools. Research shows prunes may also help protect bone density in women after menopause. They provide vitamin K, potassium and boron. Prunes are naturally sweet and filling.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Sorbitol
  • Vitamin K
  • Potassium
  • Boron

Benefits explained

Constipation (Well researched)

Clinical trials found prunes worked as well as or better than psyllium fiber for constipation.

Bone health (Some research)

Eating 5–6 prunes daily helped prevent bone loss in postmenopausal women in a 1-year trial.

Fullness (Some research)

Prunes as a snack increased fullness and supported weight management in a study.

How to use it

  • Eat 5–6 as a snack.
  • Chop into oatmeal.
  • Stew with spices.
  • Add to tagines and stews.

How much to eat

One serving: 5–6 prunes (about 50 g).

Choosing & storing

Store in an airtight container in a cool place for up to 6 months.

Safety & cautions

Large amounts can cause gas and loose stools. Increase slowly.
