Sapodilla

Manilkara zapota · Sapotaceae · In season: Winter to spring

What it does for you

Sapodilla, or chikoo, is a brown tropical fruit with sweet, malty flesh like brown sugar. It is rich in fiber and natural sugars. It provides vitamin C, potassium and copper. Its tree sap, chicle, was the original chewing gum base. It is a popular milkshake in India.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium
  • Copper

Benefits explained

Energy (Traditional use)

Provides quick natural energy.

Digestion (Some research)

High fiber supports regularity.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains tannins and polyphenols.

How to use it

  • Scoop the flesh when soft.
  • Blend into chikoo milkshakes.
  • Add to fruit salads.
  • Make ice cream.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 fruit (about 170 g).

Choosing & storing

Ripe sapodillas yield to gentle pressure.

Safety & cautions

Don't eat the seeds, which have a hook that can lodge in the throat.
