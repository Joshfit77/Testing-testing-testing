Pineapple

Ananas comosus · Bromeliaceae · In season: Spring to summer

What it does for you

Pineapple is a sweet, tangy tropical fruit full of vitamin C and manganese. It is the only major source of bromelain, an enzyme that digests protein. Bromelain has been studied for reducing swelling and inflammation. Pineapple supports immunity and bone health. It is refreshing fresh, grilled or in smoothies.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Manganese
  • Bromelain
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin B6

Benefits explained

Swelling and inflammation (Some research)

Bromelain supplements reduced swelling after surgery and injuries in several studies.

Protein digestion (Some research)

Bromelain breaks down proteins, easing digestion.

Immunity (Some research)

One cup provides about a day's vitamin C.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh chunks.
  • Grill slices.
  • Add to stir-fries and salsas.
  • Blend into smoothies.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup chunks (about 165 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose fragrant, heavy pineapples. Store at room temperature for a day or two, then refrigerate cut fruit.

Safety & cautions

Bromelain can make the mouth tingle or sore. Bromelain supplements may increase bleeding with blood thinners.
