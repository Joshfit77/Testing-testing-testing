Pineapple

Ananas comosus

What it is

Pineapple is a fruit from the Bromeliaceae family (plant family), known botanically as Ananas comosus. In season: spring to summer.

Pineapple is a sweet, tangy tropical fruit full of vitamin C and manganese. It is the only major source of bromelain, an enzyme that digests protein. Bromelain has been studied for reducing swelling and inflammation. Pineapple supports immunity and bone health. It is refreshing fresh, grilled or in smoothies.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Manganese
  • Bromelain
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin B6

Potential benefits

Swelling and inflammation (Some research)

Bromelain supplements reduced swelling after surgery and injuries in several studies.

Protein digestion (Some research)

Bromelain breaks down proteins, easing digestion.

Immunity (Some research)

One cup provides about a day's vitamin C.

Best ways to eat it

  • Eat fresh chunks.
  • Grill slices.
  • Add to stir-fries and salsas.
  • Blend into smoothies.

Typical serving

1 cup chunks (about 165 g).

When to eat it

With or after meals, or as a between-meal snack.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • People supporting digestion and regularity
  • Anyone eating to support joint comfort and calmer inflammation
  • Anyone supporting their immune system, especially in cold season

Possible downsides

  • Bromelain can make the mouth tingle or sore. Bromelain supplements may increase bleeding with blood thinners.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

Pregnant or trying to conceive: Normal amounts are safe; avoid bromelain supplements.
