Boysenberry

Rubus ursinus × idaeus · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Boysenberries are large, juicy hybrid berries made from blackberries and raspberries. They are rich in anthocyanins, fiber and vitamin C. Studies suggest boysenberry polyphenols may support lung and blood vessel health. They are sweet-tart and delicious in pies and jams. They were made famous by Knott's Berry Farm in California.

Key nutrients

  • Anthocyanins
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin K

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Very high in anthocyanins.

Blood vessels (Some research)

Boysenberry juice improved blood vessel function in animal studies.

Digestion (Traditional use)

High fiber.

How to use it

  • Bake into pies.
  • Make jam.
  • Top yogurt.
  • Blend into smoothies.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup (about 132 g).

Choosing & storing

Usually sold frozen or as jam.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
