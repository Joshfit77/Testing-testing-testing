Goldenberry

Physalis peruviana · Solanaceae (nightshade family) · In season: Year-round

What it does for you

Goldenberries, or cape gooseberries, are small golden fruits wrapped in a papery husk. They taste sweet-tart like pineapple and tomato. They are rich in vitamin A, vitamin C and withanolides. Dried goldenberries make a tangy snack. They are beautiful as a garnish.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin A
  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Withanolides

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contain withanolides and polyphenols.

Immunity (Some research)

Good source of vitamin C.

Eye health (Some research)

Rich in beta-carotene.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh after removing the husk.
  • Dip in chocolate.
  • Add dried berries to trail mix.
  • Make jam.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup (about 140 g).

Choosing & storing

Store in the husk at room temperature or refrigerate.

Safety & cautions

Unripe berries and husks are toxic — only eat ripe golden fruit.
