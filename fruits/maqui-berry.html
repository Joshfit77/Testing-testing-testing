Maqui Berry

Aristotelia chilensis · Elaeocarpaceae · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Maqui berries are deep purple berries from Patagonia in Chile. They are among the richest known sources of anthocyanins, especially delphinidins. Studies suggest maqui may help balance blood sugar. It may also support eye comfort and dry eyes. It is usually sold as powder or juice.

Key nutrients

  • Anthocyanins (delphinidins)
  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber

Benefits explained

Blood sugar (Some research)

A maqui extract lowered blood sugar in people with prediabetes in a small study.

Dry eyes (Some research)

Maqui extract improved dry eye symptoms in small trials.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Very high anthocyanin content.

How to use it

  • Add powder to smoothies.
  • Stir into yogurt or oatmeal.
  • Drink diluted juice.
  • Bake into treats.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 tsp powder (about 5 g).

Choosing & storing

Store powder airtight.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
