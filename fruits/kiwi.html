Kiwi

Actinidia deliciosa · Actinidiaceae · In season: Winter

What it does for you

Kiwifruit is a small fuzzy fruit with bright green flesh and more vitamin C than an orange. Two kiwis a day improved constipation in several clinical trials. They contain actinidin, an enzyme that helps digest protein. A study found eating two kiwis before bed improved sleep. Kiwis are also rich in vitamin K, vitamin E and fiber.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin K
  • Vitamin E
  • Fiber
  • Actinidin

Benefits explained

Constipation (Well researched)

Several trials found two green kiwifruit daily relieved constipation as well as prunes or psyllium, with less discomfort.

Sleep (Some research)

Eating two kiwis an hour before bed improved sleep onset and duration over four weeks in a small study.

Protein digestion (Some research)

Actinidin helps break down proteins, easing heaviness after meals.

How to use it

  • Halve and scoop with a spoon.
  • Slice into fruit salads.
  • Blend into green smoothies.
  • Use to tenderize meat in marinades.

How much to eat

One serving: 2 kiwis (about 150 g).

Choosing & storing

Ripe kiwis give slightly when pressed. Ripen at room temperature, then refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

Safety & cautions

Kiwi is a common allergen and may cross-react with latex. Actinidin makes kiwi curdle dairy and stops gelatin from setting.
