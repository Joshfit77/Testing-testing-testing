Strawberry

Fragaria × ananassa · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Spring to summer

What it does for you

Strawberries are America's favorite berry and one of the best fruit sources of vitamin C. One cup provides more vitamin C than an orange. Their anthocyanins and ellagic acid support heart health. Studies suggest strawberries can lower cholesterol and inflammation. They are low in sugar and delicious in endless ways.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Manganese
  • Folate
  • Fiber
  • Anthocyanins

Benefits explained

Heart health (Some research)

Daily strawberries lowered LDL cholesterol and inflammation in several small trials.

Blood sugar (Some research)

Strawberries reduced the blood sugar spike after a starchy meal.

Skin (Some research)

Vitamin C supports collagen production.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh.
  • Slice into salads with spinach and balsamic.
  • Blend into smoothies.
  • Dip in dark chocolate for a treat.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup halves (about 152 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose bright red berries with fresh green caps. Refrigerate unwashed and eat within 3 days.

Safety & cautions

Strawberries can trigger allergy and are often high in pesticide residues — rinse well or choose organic.
