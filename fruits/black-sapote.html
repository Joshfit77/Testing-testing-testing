Black Sapote

Diospyros nigra

What it is

Black Sapote is a fruit from the Ebenaceae family (ebony family), known botanically as Diospyros nigra. In season: winter.

Black sapote is the 'chocolate pudding fruit,' with soft dark flesh that tastes like chocolate custard. It is related to persimmon. It is very rich in vitamin C. It is low in fat and makes a healthy dessert. It must be eaten very ripe and soft.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Potassium

Potential benefits

Immunity (Some research)

Very high in vitamin C.

Healthy dessert (Traditional use)

A naturally low-fat chocolate-like treat.

Digestion (Some research)

Provides fiber.

Best ways to eat it

  • Scoop when very soft.
  • Blend with milk for a 'chocolate' smoothie.
  • Use in baking instead of chocolate.
  • Make mousse.

Typical serving

½ cup (about 100 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone supporting their immune system, especially in cold season
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy

Possible downsides

  • Unripe black sapote is bitter and irritating.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
