Kiwano (Horned Melon)

Cucumis metuliferus

What it is

Kiwano (Horned Melon) is a fruit from the Cucurbitaceae family (gourd family), known botanically as Cucumis metuliferus. In season: summer.

Kiwano is a spiky orange melon with bright green, jelly-like flesh. It tastes like cucumber, banana and lime. It is very hydrating and low in calories. It provides vitamin C, magnesium and iron. It makes a striking, refreshing snack.

Key nutrients

  • Water
  • Vitamin C
  • Magnesium
  • Iron

Potential benefits

Hydration (Traditional use)

Kiwano is mostly water and refreshing in heat.

Magnesium (Some research)

Provides magnesium for muscles and nerves.

Skin (Traditional use)

Vitamin C supports collagen.

Best ways to eat it

  • Halve and scoop the jelly.
  • Add to smoothies.
  • Use in salsas.
  • Garnish drinks.

Typical serving

½ kiwano (about 100 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy

Possible downsides

  • Generally safe.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
