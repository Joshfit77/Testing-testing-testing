Mangosteen

Garcinia mangostana · Clusiaceae · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Mangosteen is the 'queen of fruits,' with a purple rind and sweet, tangy white segments. Its rind is rich in xanthones, antioxidants studied for calming inflammation. The flesh provides vitamin C and fiber. Its delicate, peach-and-lychee flavor is prized across Southeast Asia. It is a rare and special treat.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Folate
  • Xanthones (rind)

Benefits explained

Anti-inflammatory (Some research)

Mangosteen xanthones reduce inflammation in laboratory studies and small trials.

Skin (Some research)

A small trial found mangosteen extract improved skin elasticity.

Immunity (Traditional use)

Provides vitamin C.

How to use it

  • Twist open the rind and eat the white segments.
  • Add to fruit salads.
  • Blend into smoothies.
  • Serve chilled as a dessert.

How much to eat

One serving: ½ cup segments (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose fruit with a deep purple rind that gives slightly. Refrigerate and eat within a few days.

Safety & cautions

Concentrated mangosteen supplements may slow blood clotting. Do not eat the rind.
