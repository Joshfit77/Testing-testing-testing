Açaí

Euterpe oleracea · Arecaceae (palm family) · In season: Year-round (frozen)

What it does for you

Açaí berries are small purple palm fruits from the Amazon. They are rich in anthocyanins and healthy fats. Açaí is usually sold as frozen pulp or powder for smoothie bowls. Small studies suggest it may improve cholesterol and antioxidant levels. Choose unsweetened açaí for the most benefit.

Key nutrients

  • Anthocyanins
  • Healthy fats
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin A

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Açaí pulp raised antioxidant levels in the blood in a small study.

Cholesterol (Some research)

A small trial found açaí pulp improved cholesterol in overweight adults.

Energy (Traditional use)

Traditionally eaten as a staple food in the Amazon.

How to use it

  • Blend frozen pulp into a smoothie bowl.
  • Top with fruit, nuts and seeds.
  • Add powder to smoothies or oatmeal.
  • Mix into yogurt.

How much to eat

One serving: 100 g frozen pulp (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Buy unsweetened frozen packs or pure powder. Keep frozen.

Safety & cautions

Many açaí bowls are loaded with sugar — choose unsweetened and watch toppings.
