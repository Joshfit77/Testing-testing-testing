Serviceberry (Saskatoon)

Amelanchier alnifolia · Rosaceae (rose family) · In season: Early summer

What it does for you

Serviceberries, or Saskatoon berries, are sweet North American berries with a nutty almond flavor. They are rich in fiber, iron and manganese. They are full of anthocyanins. They were a key ingredient in Indigenous pemmican. They are delicious fresh or baked.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Iron
  • Manganese
  • Anthocyanins

Benefits explained

Antioxidants (Some research)

Rich in anthocyanins.

Iron (Traditional use)

Good fruit source of iron.

Digestion (Traditional use)

High fiber.

How to use it

  • Eat fresh.
  • Bake into pies.
  • Make jam.
  • Add to pancakes.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup (about 145 g).

Choosing & storing

Refrigerate and use quickly, or freeze.

Safety & cautions

Seeds contain small amounts of cyanide-forming compounds — eat whole berries normally but don't grind large amounts of seeds.
