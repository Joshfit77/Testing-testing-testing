Gooseberry

Ribes uva-crispa · Grossulariaceae (currant family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Gooseberries are tart, translucent berries popular in European cooking. They are rich in vitamin C and fiber. Their tart flavor comes from natural acids that brighten desserts and sauces. They contain antioxidant polyphenols. They are delicious in jams, crumbles and fools.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin A
  • Polyphenols

Benefits explained

Immunity (Some research)

Gooseberries provide a good dose of vitamin C.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Their fiber supports regularity.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contain flavonols that help protect cells.

How to use it

  • Cook into a crumble or pie.
  • Make gooseberry fool with cream.
  • Add to sauces for fish or pork.
  • Eat ripe dessert varieties fresh.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup (about 150 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose firm berries; dessert varieties soften when ripe. Refrigerate for up to a week.

Safety & cautions

Very tart unripe. Generally safe.
