Lúcuma

Pouteria lucuma · Sapotaceae · In season: Year-round (powder)

What it does for you

Lúcuma is a Peruvian fruit with sweet, maple-like flesh. It is usually sold as powder and used as a natural sweetener. It provides fiber, beta-carotene and minerals. Its low glycemic index makes it gentler on blood sugar than sugar. It is Peru's most popular ice cream flavor.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Beta-carotene
  • Iron
  • Zinc

Benefits explained

Blood sugar (Some research)

Lúcuma extracts slowed sugar-digesting enzymes in laboratory studies.

Natural sweetener (Traditional use)

Adds sweetness with fiber.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains polyphenols and carotenoids.

How to use it

  • Blend powder into smoothies.
  • Stir into oatmeal.
  • Bake into treats.
  • Make ice cream.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 tbsp powder (about 10 g).

Choosing & storing

Store powder airtight in a cool place.

Safety & cautions

Generally safe.
