Lúcuma

Pouteria lucuma

What it is

Lúcuma is a fruit from the Sapotaceae family (plant family), known botanically as Pouteria lucuma. In season: year-round (powder).

Lúcuma is a Peruvian fruit with sweet, maple-like flesh. It is usually sold as powder and used as a natural sweetener. It provides fiber, beta-carotene and minerals. Its low glycemic index makes it gentler on blood sugar than sugar. It is Peru's most popular ice cream flavor.

Key nutrients

  • Fiber
  • Beta-carotene
  • Iron
  • Zinc

Potential benefits

Blood sugar (Some research)

Lúcuma extracts slowed sugar-digesting enzymes in laboratory studies.

Natural sweetener (Traditional use)

Adds sweetness with fiber.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains polyphenols and carotenoids.

Best ways to eat it

  • Blend powder into smoothies.
  • Stir into oatmeal.
  • Bake into treats.
  • Make ice cream.

Typical serving

1 tbsp powder (about 10 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy

Possible downsides

  • Generally safe.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
