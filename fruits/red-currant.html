Red Currant

Ribes rubrum · Grossulariaceae (currant family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Red currants are glossy, jewel-like berries with a bright, tart flavor. They are rich in vitamin C and fiber. They contain antioxidant anthocyanins and flavonols. They are classic in jellies, sauces and summer puddings. Their beauty makes them a favorite garnish.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Fiber
  • Vitamin K
  • Anthocyanins

Benefits explained

Immunity (Some research)

A good source of vitamin C.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Provides fiber.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Contains flavonols like quercetin.

How to use it

  • Make red currant jelly.
  • Garnish desserts.
  • Add to summer pudding.
  • Sprinkle on yogurt.

How much to eat

One serving: 1 cup (about 112 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose plump, glossy berries on the stem. Refrigerate and use within a few days.

Safety & cautions

Very tart; generally safe.
