Cherimoya

Annona cherimola

What it is

Cherimoya is a fruit from the Annonaceae family (custard apple family), known botanically as Annona cherimola. In season: winter to spring.

Cherimoya, or custard apple, has creamy flesh that tastes like banana, pineapple and vanilla. Mark Twain called it 'the most delicious fruit known to men.' It provides vitamin C, vitamin B6 and fiber. It is rich in natural sugars for energy. It is best eaten chilled with a spoon.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin B6
  • Fiber
  • Potassium

Potential benefits

Energy (Traditional use)

Natural sugars provide quick energy.

Mood and nerves (Some research)

Vitamin B6 supports the production of brain messengers like serotonin.

Digestion (Traditional use)

Provides fiber.

Best ways to eat it

  • Halve and scoop with a spoon.
  • Blend into smoothies.
  • Freeze into sorbet.
  • Add to fruit salads.

Typical serving

½ cherimoya (about 160 g).

When to eat it

Morning, or as a snack before activity, for natural energy.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone working toward more fruits and vegetables each day
  • Anyone wanting steadier energy
  • People supporting digestion and regularity

Possible downsides

  • Never eat the seeds or skin — they contain toxic compounds.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

As a food, it is generally considered fine during pregnancy and breastfeeding as part of a varied diet. Wash well before eating.
