Lychee

Litchi chinensis · Sapindaceae (soapberry family) · In season: Summer

What it does for you

Lychees are fragrant tropical fruits with bumpy red shells and juicy white flesh. They are rich in vitamin C, with just a handful providing a day's worth. They contain polyphenols such as epicatechin and rutin. Their floral sweetness makes them a popular summer treat in Asia. They are delicious fresh or in desserts.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C
  • Copper
  • Polyphenols
  • Potassium

Benefits explained

Immunity (Some research)

About 10 lychees provide a full day's vitamin C.

Antioxidants (Some research)

Lychee polyphenols protect cells in laboratory studies.

Hydration (Traditional use)

Lychees are juicy and refreshing in hot weather.

How to use it

  • Peel and eat fresh.
  • Add to fruit salads.
  • Blend into smoothies or sorbet.
  • Use in cocktails and mocktails.

How much to eat

One serving: 10 lychees (about 100 g).

Choosing & storing

Choose bright red, heavy lychees. Refrigerate for up to a week.

Safety & cautions

Never eat unripe lychees on an empty stomach — unripe fruit contains compounds that can drop blood sugar dangerously, especially in undernourished children.
