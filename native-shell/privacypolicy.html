Privacy Policy

This page explains what Narrow Gate Fitness does with your information, including health data from Apple Health and Health Connect.

Health data (steps)

  • If you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), the app reads your daily step count only. It does not read any other health data and never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Steps are used only to show your daily steps and step history inside the app.
  • We never sell health data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.
  • You can turn access off at any time: on iPhone in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Narrow Gate Fitness; on Android in the Health Connect app > App permissions.

Offline edition

In the offline edition everything you log (workouts, food, supplements, steps, photos, notes) is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Account edition

If you use a Narrow Gate Fitness account, the data you log, including synced daily step totals, is stored in your account so it appears on all your devices. Only you can see it. You can delete entries, or your whole account, from the Profile screen.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the Narrow Gate Fitness team through the support details in the app listing.
