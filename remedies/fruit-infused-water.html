Fruit & Herb Infused Water

If plain water feels boring, a little fruit and fresh herbs make it taste like a treat — with almost no sugar.

What it may help with

  • Drinking more water through the day
  • Cutting back on soda and sweet drinks

Ingredients

  • 2 quarts (2 L) cold water
  • ½ cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 handful fresh mint leaves, lightly crushed

How to make it

  1. Wash the cucumber, lemon and mint well.
  2. Add them to a large pitcher.
  3. Fill with cold water and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  4. Refill the pitcher with water once or twice, then start fresh.

Suggested amount

A glass at a time; most adults need about 8–12 cups of fluids a day from all drinks and foods.

How often

Throughout the day.

Evidence level: Traditional use

Flavoring water is a practical way to drink more; staying hydrated supports energy, focus and digestion.

Safety warnings

  • Use within 24 hours and keep refrigerated.
  • Citrus is acidic — rinse with plain water to protect tooth enamel.
  • If you're on a fluid restriction for heart or kidney disease, follow your doctor's limit.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Constant thirst with frequent urination (possible high blood sugar)
  • Signs of dehydration that don't improve with drinking
