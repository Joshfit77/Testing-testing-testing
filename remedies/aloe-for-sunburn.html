Aloe Vera for Mild Sunburn

The clear gel inside aloe leaves is cooling and moisturizing, which is why it's a classic for mild sunburn.

What it may help with

  • Mild sunburn
  • Minor skin irritation

Ingredients

  • 1 aloe leaf or pure aloe vera gel (look for few added ingredients and no alcohol)

How to make it

  1. If using a fresh leaf, wash it, slice it open and scoop out only the clear gel (avoid the yellow sap just under the skin).
  2. Cool your skin first with a cool shower or damp cloth.
  3. Gently spread a thin layer of gel over the sunburn.
  4. Chill the gel in the fridge for extra cooling.

Suggested amount

A thin layer.

How often

2–3 times a day.

Evidence level: Some research

Aloe gel is widely used for minor burns. Some studies suggest it helps minor burns heal, but results for sunburn are mixed.

Safety warnings

  • For skin use only — don't swallow aloe latex (the yellow sap), which is a strong laxative.
  • Stop if you get a rash or more redness; people allergic to onions, garlic or tulips may react.
  • Don't use on deep or open wounds.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Blistering over a large area
  • Fever, chills, headache or confusion with sunburn
  • Severe pain, or signs of dehydration
  • Signs of infection: increasing redness, warmth, pus or red streaks

For education only — not medical advice.
