Tart Cherry Juice Before Bed

Tart (Montmorency) cherries naturally contain small amounts of melatonin, the body's sleep hormone, plus compounds that help the body use tryptophan.

What it may help with

  • Getting a little more sleep
  • Muscle soreness after exercise

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (240 ml) unsweetened tart cherry juice — or 2 tbsp (30 ml) tart cherry concentrate mixed into 1 cup of water

How to make it

  1. Pour the juice (or stir the concentrate into water).
  2. Drink one glass in the morning and one about 1–2 hours before bed, as in the studies.
  3. Brush your teeth afterwards — it's acidic and sweet.

Suggested amount

1 cup (240 ml) of juice.

How often

Twice a day (morning and 1–2 hours before bed) for 1–2 weeks, as studied.

Evidence level: Some research

Small studies found tart cherry juice modestly increased total sleep time in adults with insomnia and older adults.

Safety warnings

  • Juice is high in natural sugar and calories — count it if you manage blood sugar or weight.
  • Can loosen stools in some people.
  • Choose unsweetened juice.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Insomnia lasting more than a month
  • Daytime sleepiness that affects driving or work
  • Loud snoring or gasping during sleep
