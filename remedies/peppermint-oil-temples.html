Peppermint Oil Temple Rub

Peppermint oil creates a cooling feeling on the skin that may dull pain signals. Diluted peppermint oil has been studied for tension headaches.

What it may help with

  • Tension headaches
  • A tight, pressing feeling across the forehead

Ingredients

  • 2 drops peppermint essential oil
  • 1 tsp (5 ml) carrier oil, such as olive, almond or jojoba oil

How to make it

  1. Mix the peppermint oil into the carrier oil in a small dish.
  2. Dab a little on your fingertips and gently massage it into your temples and forehead, and the back of your neck.
  3. Keep it well away from your eyes, and wash your hands afterwards.
  4. Rest in a quiet room. You can reapply after 15 and 30 minutes.

Suggested amount

A fingertip of the diluted oil.

How often

Up to 3 applications per headache.

Evidence level: Some research

Small trials found a 10% peppermint oil solution on the temples eased tension headaches about as well as a standard dose of acetaminophen.

Safety warnings

  • Never use peppermint oil on or near the face of babies or young children — it can affect their breathing.
  • Keep away from the eyes; if it gets in, rinse with plain water or milk.
  • Patch-test on your arm first — it can irritate sensitive skin.
  • Never swallow essential oils.

When to seek medical care instead

  • A sudden, severe "worst headache of my life" — call 911
  • Headache with fever and a stiff neck, confusion, weakness, numbness, trouble speaking or vision loss
  • A headache after a head injury
  • New headaches after age 50, or headaches that keep getting worse
  • In pregnancy: a headache with vision changes or swelling of the face or hands
