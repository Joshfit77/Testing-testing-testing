Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)

Slow, even breathing is a free, always-available remedy. Long, steady breaths may activate the body's "rest and digest" nervous system.

What it may help with

  • Calming a racing heart or mind
  • Stress before a hard conversation or test
  • Winding down before sleep

Ingredients

  • 2–5 minutes in a quiet spot (sitting or lying down)

How to make it

  1. Sit comfortably with your feet on the floor, or lie down. Relax your shoulders.
  2. Breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of 4.
  3. Hold your breath gently for a count of 4.
  4. Breathe out slowly through your mouth for a count of 4.
  5. Hold for a count of 4, then repeat. Do 4–6 rounds, or continue for up to 5 minutes.

Suggested amount

4–6 rounds (about 2–5 minutes).

How often

As often as needed, several times a day.

Evidence level: Some research

Slow, paced breathing has been studied for lowering stress, heart rate and blood pressure. The studies are promising but mostly small.

Safety warnings

  • If you feel dizzy or lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.
  • Skip the breath holds if you're pregnant or have a heart or lung condition — just breathe slowly in and out.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting — call 911
  • Frequent panic attacks
  • Thoughts of harming yourself — call or text 988 (U.S.)
