Honey & Lemon Warm Water

Honey coats and soothes the throat, and it has more research for coughs than most home remedies.

What it may help with

  • A sore, scratchy throat
  • Coughing, especially at night (ages 1 and up)

Ingredients

  • 1–2 tsp honey
  • 1 cup (240 ml) warm water or caffeine-free tea
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

How to make it

  1. Warm the water until comfortably hot (not boiling).
  2. Stir in the honey until dissolved.
  3. Add the lemon juice.
  4. Sip slowly while warm.

Suggested amount

1 cup, with 1–2 tsp honey. For a nighttime cough: ½ tsp honey for ages 1–5, 1 tsp for ages 6–11, 2 tsp for 12 and up, at bedtime.

How often

Up to 3–4 times a day.

Evidence level: Well researched

Reviews of studies found honey may ease cough symptoms and help sleep in children over 1 and adults, about as well as some over-the-counter cough medicines.

Safety warnings

  • Never give honey to babies under 1 year — it can cause infant botulism.
  • Honey is a sugar; count it if you manage blood sugar.
  • Lemon is acidic — rinse your mouth with plain water afterwards to protect tooth enamel.

When to seek medical care instead

  • A cough lasting more than 3 weeks, or coughing up blood
  • Shortness of breath, wheezing, or chest pain
  • High fever, or a baby under 3 months with any fever
