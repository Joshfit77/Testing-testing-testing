Warm Heat Pack for Cramps

Warmth relaxes tight muscles and improves blood flow. It's simple, drug-free and surprisingly effective.

What it may help with

  • Period cramps
  • Lower back aches during your period

Ingredients

  • 1 heating pad on low, a hot water bottle, or a microwavable heat pack
  • 1 thin towel or cloth

How to make it

  1. Fill a hot water bottle with warm (not boiling) water, or warm a heat pack as directed.
  2. Wrap it in a thin towel.
  3. Place it on your lower belly or lower back while you rest.
  4. Leave it on for about 20 minutes, then take a break.

Suggested amount

20 minutes at a time.

How often

As needed throughout the day, with breaks for your skin.

Evidence level: Some research

Small studies found continuous low-level heat eased period cramps about as well as some pain relievers.

Safety warnings

  • Always use a cloth layer and a low setting to avoid burns.
  • Don't fall asleep with a heating pad on.
  • Use extra care if you have diabetes or reduced feeling in your skin.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Severe pain, or pain that is getting worse over time
  • Very heavy bleeding or bleeding between periods
  • Pain with fever
