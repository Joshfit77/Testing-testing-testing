Fennel Seeds After Meals

In India, a small spoonful of fennel seeds after a meal is a centuries-old habit. The seeds' oils may help relax the gut and ease gas.

What it may help with

  • Gas after meals
  • Bloating
  • Freshening the breath

Ingredients

  • ½ tsp fennel seeds (plain or lightly toasted)

How to make it

  1. After your meal, put about half a teaspoon of fennel seeds in your mouth.
  2. Chew slowly for a minute or two to release the sweet, licorice-like oils.
  3. Or, crush 1 tsp of seeds and steep in 1 cup of just-boiled water for 10 minutes as a tea.

Suggested amount

½ tsp to chew, or 1 cup of tea made with 1 tsp crushed seeds.

How often

After meals, up to 3 times a day.

Evidence level: Traditional use

Fennel is a traditional digestive aid in many cultures. A few small studies suggest benefits for gas and infant colic, but research in adults is limited.

Safety warnings

  • Avoid if you're allergic to celery, carrot or mugwort (related plants).
  • Avoid large amounts and fennel oil during pregnancy.
  • Don't give fennel oil or concentrated fennel tea to babies without your pediatrician's guidance.

When to seek medical care instead

  • Ongoing belly pain or a big change in bowel habits
  • Blood in the stool
  • Bloating with unexplained weight loss
