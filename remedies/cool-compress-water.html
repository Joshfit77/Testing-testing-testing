Cool Compress & a Big Glass of Water

Not drinking enough is a common headache trigger, and cold therapy may calm throbbing pain. This simple combination is a good first step.

What it may help with

  • Headaches from dehydration, heat or a long day
  • Throbbing headaches

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (480 ml) water
  • 1 clean washcloth soaked in cool water, or an ice pack wrapped in a thin towel

How to make it

  1. Drink 2 cups of water slowly over 10–15 minutes.
  2. Lie down in a dark, quiet room.
  3. Place the cool cloth or wrapped ice pack on your forehead, temples or the back of your neck.
  4. Rest for 15–20 minutes, then remove the compress.

Suggested amount

2 cups of water and one 15–20 minute compress.

How often

Up to every 1–2 hours, with breaks for your skin.

Evidence level: Some research

Small studies found that drinking more water eased headaches in some people prone to them, and cold therapy may reduce migraine pain.

Safety warnings

  • Always wrap ice in a cloth and limit it to 20 minutes to protect your skin.
  • If you have heart or kidney disease and limit fluids, follow your doctor's fluid advice.

When to seek medical care instead

  • A sudden, severe headache — call 911
  • Headache with fever, stiff neck, confusion, weakness or vision changes
  • Headaches that wake you from sleep or are getting more frequent
