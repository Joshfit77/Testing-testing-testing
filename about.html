Our Story

Beauty & Praise celebrates the gifts of creation and the simple, grateful habits that help us flourish.

Why we began

Beauty in every leaf, praise in every day

For generations, families have turned to the garden and the kitchen cupboard for comfort — a cup of chamomile at bedtime, ginger for an upset stomach, thyme and honey for a cough. These traditions carry wisdom and beauty worth remembering.

Beauty & Praise was created to gather that knowledge in one welcoming place, with clear information on how each herb is traditionally used, how to prepare it, and — just as importantly — when to be careful.

Alongside our herb library, we share gentle daily reminders: drink water, step into the sunshine, rest well, and give thanks. Small steps, taken faithfully, add up to a healthier life.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the information on this site medical advice?

No. Everything here is for general education. Herbs can interact with medicines and aren't right for everyone. Please talk with your doctor or pharmacist before using herbs medicinally, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medicine, or caring for a child.

Where does your herb information come from?

Our profiles bring together long-standing traditional use with findings from published research, described in plain language. Where evidence is limited or mixed, we say so.

How do the daily checklist and water tracker work?

They save your progress in your own browser, on your device only. Nothing is sent to us. Your checklist and water count reset each morning.

How do I save my favorite herbs?

Tap the heart on any herb card or profile. Find all your saved herbs using the heart icon in the top menu.

Do you sell herbs or supplements?

Not at this time. We recommend buying from reputable suppliers that test their products for quality and correct identification.
