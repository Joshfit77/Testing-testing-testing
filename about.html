Our Story

Beauty & Praise helps you use real food, natural remedies, herbs and simple, grateful habits to support everyday wellness.

Why we began

Food first, faith always

For generations, families have turned to the kitchen for comfort and strength — a pot of soup when someone was sick, oatmeal and fruit to start the day, ginger for an upset stomach, honey for a cough. Real food has always been the foundation of a healthy home.

Beauty & Praise was created to gather that wisdom in one welcoming place: everyday foods and what they offer, simple natural remedies with exact amounts, and herbs as a supporting tool — always with practical guidance on how much, how often, and when to be careful.

Alongside our food, remedy and herb guides, we share gentle daily reminders: drink water, step into the sunshine, rest well, and give thanks. Small steps, taken faithfully, add up to a healthier life.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the information on this site medical advice?

No. Everything here is for general education. Foods, remedies and herbs can interact with medicines and aren't right for everyone. Please talk with your doctor or pharmacist before using herbs medicinally, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medicine, or caring for a child.

Where does your information come from?

Our profiles bring together long-standing traditional use with findings from published research, described in plain language. Food pages list their references, such as USDA FoodData Central and NIH nutrient fact sheets. Where evidence is limited or mixed, we say so.

How do the daily checklist and water tracker work?

They save your progress in your own browser, on your device only. Nothing is sent to us. Your checklist and water count reset each morning.

How do I save my favorite foods and herbs?

Tap the heart on any food, fruit or herb card or profile. Find everything you've saved in My Plan, using the heart icon in the top menu.

Do you sell herbs or supplements?

Not at this time. We recommend buying from reputable suppliers that test their products for quality and correct identification.
