Beets

Vegetables

What it is

Beets are deep red root vegetables rich in natural nitrates, folate and betalain pigments.

Key nutrients

  • Dietary nitrates
  • Folate
  • Fiber
  • Potassium
  • Betalains

Potential benefits

Blood pressure (Well researched)

Beet juice nitrates are turned into nitric oxide, which helps relax blood vessels; trials found modest drops in blood pressure.

Exercise stamina (Some research)

Beet juice taken 2–3 hours before exercise has been studied for improving endurance in some people.

Antioxidant support (Some research)

Betalain pigments act as antioxidants.

Best ways to eat it

  • Roast whole wrapped in foil, then peel and slice.
  • Grate raw into salads.
  • Blend a small piece into smoothies.
  • Pair with goat cheese and walnuts.

Typical serving

½ cup cooked (about 85 g).

When to eat it

Lunch or dinner, or 2–3 hours before a workout.

Who may benefit

  • People supporting healthy blood pressure
  • Endurance athletes

Possible downsides

  • Can harmlessly turn urine and stools pink or red.
  • High in oxalates — limit if you're prone to kidney stones.

Allergies & interactions

Allergy is rare.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

A healthy vegetable during pregnancy, providing folate.
