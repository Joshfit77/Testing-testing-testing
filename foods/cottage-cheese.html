Cottage Cheese

Dairy & fermented

What it is

Cottage cheese is a fresh, mild cheese made of soft curds. It's very high in protein, mostly slow-digesting casein.

Key nutrients

  • About 12–14 g protein per ½ cup (113 g)
  • Calcium
  • Vitamin B12
  • Selenium
  • Casein protein

Potential benefits

High-protein snack (Well researched)

Cottage cheese packs a lot of protein for few calories, which may help with fullness and muscle maintenance.

Overnight muscle repair (Some research)

Its casein protein digests slowly; a small bedtime serving has been studied for supporting overnight muscle repair in active people.

Bone support (Some research)

Provides calcium and protein for bones.

Best ways to eat it

  • Top with fruit and cinnamon.
  • Blend into pancake batter or smoothies.
  • Spread on toast with tomato and pepper.
  • Stir into scrambled eggs for creaminess.

Typical serving

½ cup (about 113 g).

When to eat it

Breakfast, a snack, or a small serving in the evening.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone wanting more protein
  • Active people and older adults maintaining muscle

Possible downsides

  • Many brands are high in sodium — compare labels.

Allergies & interactions

Contains milk, a major allergen. Has some lactose.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

A good protein source during pregnancy — choose cottage cheese made from pasteurized milk.
