Broccoli

Vegetables

What it is

Broccoli is a cruciferous vegetable rich in vitamin C, fiber and glucoraphanin, which becomes the studied compound sulforaphane when chopped or chewed.

Key nutrients

  • Vitamin C (about half a day's needs per ½ cup cooked)
  • Vitamin K
  • Folate
  • Fiber
  • Sulforaphane

Potential benefits

Immune support (Some research)

Broccoli is one of the best vegetable sources of vitamin C.

Natural detox support (Some research)

Sulforaphane has been studied for switching on the body's own antioxidant and detox enzymes.

Digestive health (Some research)

Its fiber supports regularity and gut bacteria.

Best ways to eat it

  • Roast with olive oil and garlic until crisp-edged.
  • Steam lightly to keep more vitamin C.
  • Chop and let sit 10 minutes before cooking to boost sulforaphane.
  • Add to stir-fries, soups and pasta.

Typical serving

½ cup cooked or 1 cup raw (about 78 g).

When to eat it

Lunch or dinner.

Who may benefit

  • Anyone supporting immunity
  • People wanting more fiber and vegetables

Possible downsides

  • Can cause gas in some people.

Allergies & interactions

Contains vitamin K: keep intake steady if you take warfarin.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

A nourishing vegetable during pregnancy for folate and vitamin C.
