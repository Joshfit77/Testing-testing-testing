Quinoa

Whole grains

What it is

Quinoa is a seed cooked like a grain. It's naturally gluten-free and one of the few plant foods with complete protein.

Key nutrients

  • 8 g protein per cup cooked (185 g)
  • 5 g fiber
  • Magnesium
  • Iron
  • Folate

Potential benefits

Complete plant protein (Some research)

Quinoa contains all nine essential amino acids, making it a useful protein for vegetarians.

Steady energy (Some research)

Its fiber and protein help it digest more slowly than white rice.

Minerals (Some research)

A good source of magnesium and iron, which many people fall short on.

Best ways to eat it

  • Use as a base for grain bowls.
  • Toss with vegetables and lemon dressing for a salad.
  • Cook as a warm breakfast porridge with fruit.
  • Add to soups.

Typical serving

1 cup cooked (about 185 g).

When to eat it

Lunch or dinner, or as a breakfast porridge.

Who may benefit

  • Vegetarians
  • People with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity
  • Active people

Possible downsides

  • Rinse before cooking to remove bitter saponins.
  • Contains oxalates — people prone to kidney stones may need to limit it.

Allergies & interactions

Quinoa allergy is uncommon.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

A nourishing grain during pregnancy, providing folate, iron and protein.
