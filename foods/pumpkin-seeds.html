Pumpkin Seeds

Nuts & seeds

What it is

Pumpkin seeds (pepitas) are green, hulled seeds that are one of the best food sources of magnesium and zinc.

Key nutrients

  • Magnesium (over a third of daily needs per oz)
  • Zinc
  • About 8 g protein per oz
  • Iron
  • Tryptophan

Potential benefits

Magnesium for sleep and relaxation (Some research)

Magnesium supports muscle and nerve relaxation; low magnesium has been linked with poorer sleep.

Zinc for immunity and men's health (Some research)

Zinc supports immune cells and normal testosterone production.

Prostate health (Some research)

Pumpkin seed products have been studied for easing urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate.

Best ways to eat it

  • Sprinkle on salads, soups and oatmeal.
  • Roast with a pinch of salt and spices.
  • Add to trail mix.
  • Blend into pesto.

Typical serving

1 oz (about ¼ cup) (about 28 g).

When to eat it

A small handful in the evening or as an afternoon snack.

Who may benefit

  • Men supporting zinc intake
  • People with poor sleep or muscle cramps
  • Vegetarians

Possible downsides

  • Calorie-dense; choose unsalted.

Allergies & interactions

Seed allergies are uncommon but possible.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

A healthy snack during pregnancy, providing iron, zinc and magnesium.
