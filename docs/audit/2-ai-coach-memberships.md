## 5. Launch blockers for a physical-iPhone beta / App Store submission (this area)

1. **Decide `REVENUECAT_ALLOW_SANDBOX` on the deployed server** (D2). While it is false (the code default, and what the
   older docs said for production), no sandbox purchase unlocks Pro: not the sandbox test plan, not TestFlight, and
   not Apple's App Review purchase test. While it is true, every TestFlight tester gets Pro free. A per-account
   allow-list would avoid that trade-off but isn't built.
2. **Store and RevenueCat setup** (owner): App Store Connect subscription group with `ngf_pro_monthly` $19.99 and `ngf_pro_yearly` $149.99, RevenueCat project, entitlement `pro`, offering, webhook with its `Authorization` value, and the `REVENUECAT_*` variables; the In-App Purchase capability in Xcode. Until `REVENUECAT_SECRET_API_KEY` is set the iOS billing screen shows "In-app purchases aren't available in the iOS app yet" and sells nothing (fine for a beta, not for submission of a paid app).
3. **An Anthropic key with a spend limit** (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`); without it the coach says it isn't configured (verified), which is acceptable for a beta but the AI rows of the paywall would then describe an unavailable feature.
4. **Sandbox device run** of `docs/MOBILE_BILLING.md`'s plan (purchase, switch, cancel, restore, refund, billing problem) — BLOCKED here.
5. Final legal documents (`LEGAL_TERMS_URL` / `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL`), covered by the legal audit.

- Real RevenueCat webhooks, App Store sandbox states (grace, billing retry, refund timing), Restore across reinstall, Google Play base plans: a RevenueCat project, store products, sandbox testers and a physical iPhone / Android device.
- Stripe test-mode checkout, portal plan switching, refund and dispute events: Stripe test keys and two test prices.
- Route `maxDuration` (180 s chat, 60 s photo) on the real host: whether the hosting plan allows functions that long
  (not checked here; if it caps lower, long coach turns and plan generation are cut off).
- The port-3000 demo server's Free/Pro switch and simulated chat in a browser: deliberately not exercised (writes to the shared demo account); covered by `demo-mode.test.ts` and the existing `e2e/demo.spec.ts`.
