## Optional improvements (not requirements)

- Add `android:allowBackup="false"` (or data-extraction rules) on Android and drop the Health Connect write permissions the plugin declares but the app never uses (`SECURITY_PRIVACY.md` findings 8–9) before any Play submission.
- Consider Sign in with Apple as a convenience even though it is not required.
- A CI assertion that the synced `ios/App/App/capacitor.config.json` points at an `https://` host before archiving (the TestFlight job already refuses placeholders).

## Google Play (for later)

Play's Health Content and Services policy (updated May 2025) and the Health apps declaration apply to a Health & Fitness app; Health Connect data counts as personal and sensitive data; the privacy policy must be at a public, non-geofenced URL. Sources: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16555673 , https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291 . The tracked Android project is also missing the background-geolocation and RevenueCat plugin wiring (`android/app/capacitor.build.gradle`), so an Android build from this tree has no GPS background mode or purchases yet.
