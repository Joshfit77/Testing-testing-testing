## 1. Scope, method and limitations

### 1.1 Scope

Candidate marks reviewed: NARROW GATE FITNESS, NARROW GATE (and NARROW GATES, NARROWGATE, NARROW GATE ATHLETICS, NARROW PATH), the tagline "Rooted in faith. Built for strength.", NUTRITION SCORE / "Narrow Gate Nutrition Score", FOOD IQ, and third-party program, method, equipment and device names that appear inside the app.

### 1.2 Method

1. Read the two earlier reports (`docs/launch-audit/C_TRADEMARKS.md`, `docs/launch-audit/FINAL_TRADEMARK_REVIEW.md`).
2. Searched the repository (`src/`, `public/`, `ios/`, `android/`, `native-shell/`, `supabase/`, `capacitor.config.ts`, legal drafts) for each mark and for third-party names.
3. Ran about 40 web searches (general search engine, standard and extended modes) for each mark and variant.

### 1.3 Limitations (read before relying on anything below)

| Limitation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **No official register could be reached.** Direct requests from this environment to `tsdr.uspto.gov`, `tmsearch.uspto.gov`, `tsdrapi.uspto.gov` failed (no connection; the page-fetch tool reported the host as outside the session's allowed network). | **There is no OFFICIAL RECORD evidence anywhere in this document.** Every USPTO fact is from a third-party mirror. |
| **No secondary database page could be opened either.** `trademarks.justia.com`, `uspto.report`, `trademarkia.com`, `tmdn.org` (TMview), `branddb.wipo.int`, `rdap.org`, `apps.apple.com`, `itunes.apple.com` and the businesses' own websites all failed the same way. | Every fact below comes from a **search-engine summary of a page**, not from the page itself. | Mirror sites lag the USPTO and disagree with each other. | Two records below show conflicting statuses on different mirrors (C-07, C-17). Neither is resolved here. |
| No search by design/logo, phonetic equivalent, or translation. No class-filtered search. | The gate/cross logo was not searched at all. |

### 1.4 Evidence labels used

- **OFFICIAL RECORD:** seen on a government register. *None in this document.*
- **SECONDARY SOURCE:** a third-party mirror of register data, or a business's own page, as summarised by a search engine. The URL is the page the summary came from.
- **UNVERIFIED LEAD:** reported by an earlier report or a directory but not seen again today, or seen only as a name with no detail.

5. Does shared meaning with "Narrow Path" marks matter where the words differ?
6. Is "Rooted in faith. Built for strength." functioning as a trademark as we use it, and how does ROOTED IN STRENGTH bear on it?
7. Is "Nutrition Score" merely descriptive? Is using it beside displayed Nutri-Score grades acceptable? May a non-operator app display Nutri-Score grades at all, and in what visual form?
8. Is the "in the style of [person]'s [method]" wording acceptable for each named method, and is there a separate name-and-likeness or endorsement issue? Should "80/20" stay in a program title?
9. Should equipment aliases such as "assault bike", "stairmaster" and "ski erg" stay, be attributed, or be replaced?
10. Options counsel may wish to weigh for the house mark, listed neutrally and without recommendation: proceed as is; proceed with a narrowed or differently structured filing; seek coexistence or consent; delay launch pending the search; adopt a different name.
