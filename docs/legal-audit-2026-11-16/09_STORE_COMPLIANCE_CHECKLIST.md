# 09 — App Store and Google Play compliance checklist

**Narrow Gate Fitness · prelaunch audit · 2026-11-16 · branch `claude/friends-challenges`**

Readers: the founder and US counsel.

**What this is.** A comparison of the current store rules with what the code in this repository does. It was prepared by an AI assistant, not a lawyer. It is not legal advice and it is not a prediction of what App Review or Google Play review will decide.

**What it cannot show.** Passing automated tests does not prove store compliance. Nothing here was run on a physical iPhone or Android phone, and no App Store Connect, Google Play Console, RevenueCat or Stripe account was inspected. Every row says which kind of check is still needed.

## Summary

- **Blocks submission today:** the Privacy Policy and Terms are placeholder drafts with unfilled brackets and no contact details; there is no support or contact address anywhere in the app; no store metadata, review notes or reviewer demo account exist yet.
- **Blocks switching Friends on:** no filter for objectionable usernames or display names, no published contact, no moderation process or response-time commitment, and Terms that do not cover conduct between members. The feature is flag-gated off, so it does not block a launch without it.
- **Sound in code, unproven on a device:** in-app purchase through RevenueCat with Restore, Manage, renewal terms and legal links; web checkout hidden and refused inside the native apps; in-app account deletion; AI data-sharing consent before anything is sent to Anthropic; read-only HealthKit / Health Connect with unused permissions removed; no tracking SDKs; Android target API 36.
- **Judgment calls for the owner and counsel:** the app is a native shell that loads the deployed website (Apple 4.2 / 2.5.2); health-related wording and the store health declarations; the age rating answers for an 18+ app with religious, health and (later) social content.

## 3. Findings

Severity: **High** = likely rejection or policy breach if shipped as is; **Medium** = likely questions or partial non-compliance; **Low** = tidy-up.

### S-1 Legal documents are placeholders; no contact details (High)
- **Confirmed.**
- **Evidence:** `src/app/legal/layout.tsx:13-16`; `privacy/page.tsx:12,49,55,61,66,71-72`; `terms/page.tsx:8,19-23`; `store-paywall.tsx:203`; `legal-links.tsx:16`; versions marked `2026-10-26-placeholder` (`src/lib/legal/versions.ts:15-16`).
- **Impact:** Apple 2.1(a) (placeholder content), 3.1.2 (Terms and Privacy links), 5.1.1(i); Google User Data policy. Members would be accepting documents that say they are not binding.
- **Action:** counsel finalises both documents (and the EULA choice); publish at stable public URLs; set `LEGAL_TERMS_URL` / `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL` or replace the pages; bump versions.
- Counsel must decide: **Y** · Blocks public launch: **Y** · Blocks paid launch: **Y** · Technical testing can resolve it: **N**

### S-2 No published support or contact information (High)
- **Confirmed** (no address, form or link found by search of `src/`, `public/`, `docs/legal/`).
- **Impact:** Apple 1.2 (when Friends is on), Support URL metadata, 5.6.2; Google developer contact and deletion-request route.

### S-3 Friends: no objectionable-content filter, no moderation process (High if the flag is switched on; none while it is off)
