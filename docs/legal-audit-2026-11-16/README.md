# Narrow Gate Fitness: prelaunch legal, trademark, copyright and IP audit

Date: 2026-11-16. Branch audited: `claude/friends-challenges` (which contains everything in
`release/testflight-rc-2026-11-15` and `claude/pro-premium`, plus the Friends system, switched off).

**This package is not legal advice.** It was prepared by an AI assistant from the repository and from whatever public
web sources could be reached. It records facts, evidence, risks and questions for the founder and licensed counsel. It
does not clear any trademark, give any opinion on patent infringement or validity, or certify compliance with any law,
licence or store policy.

**The most important limit:** the environment this audit ran in could not reach most official sources. The USPTO
trademark and patent systems, Google Patents, Espacenet, the app stores, Google Play's policy pages, Open Food Facts'
and the Open Data Commons' own sites, and the third-party businesses' websites were blocked. Where a fact rests on a
search-engine summary, a mirror site or a GitHub copy, the report says so. Counsel must re-run the official searches.
