## 6. Questions for counsel

1. Will you run or commission a full search (federal word and design, state, common law, business names, domains, app stores, social handles) for NARROW GATE FITNESS, NARROW GATE, the logo, the tagline and the feature names?
2. What are the current TSDR status, owner and services for every serial number in section 4?
3. For C-01, C-02 and C-03: first-use dates, trading areas, current activity, and what they mean for a nationwide app.
4. Does wide religious use of "narrow gate" bear on the strength of the phrase, and in which direction?
5. Does shared meaning with the "Narrow Path" names matter where the words differ?
6. Tagline: does it function as a mark as used (slogan, notification title, meta description), and how does ROOTED IN STRENGTH bear on it?
7. Nutri-Score: (a) may an app that is not a food operator display a product's Nutri-Score grade, and in what form; (b) is the letter-on-colour tile acceptable; (c) is "Narrow Gate Nutrition Score" a concern beside it; (d) is a non-affiliation line wanted?
8. Is "Nutrition Score" descriptive, and what follows for use and registration?
9. Is the "in the style of [person]'s [method]" wording acceptable for each row in 5.2? Is there a separate name-and-likeness or endorsement question? Should "80/20" and "Conjugate" stay in program titles?
10. Should the equipment aliases stay, be capitalised and attributed, or be replaced with generic terms?
11. Should the notices page credit the wearable brands and Anthropic as well as Apple and Google? Do Apple's and Google's naming rules affect any wording in 5.2?
12. If and when a filing is made: which marks, which classes (questions only: 9, 41, 42, 44, 45, 25), intent-to-use or not, and in whose name (individual now, entity later by assignment, or entity first)?
13. Does the name's visibility in a repository and in test builds affect timing?
14. Territory: US only, or wherever the stores will distribute the app?
