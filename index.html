Why the name

The harder way is usually the better one

Good design is mostly saying no: to clutter, to trends that fade in a year, to shortcuts. We take the narrow road on every project. That means fewer, better choices and work you will be proud of for a long time.

Every project starts with listening. We want to understand your people and your mission before we draw a single line.

“Enter through the narrow gate.”

Matthew 7:13

How it works

Four steps, start to finish

  1. ListenA free call to learn about you, your audience and what success looks like.
  2. SketchTwo or three directions, shown side by side, so you can pick with confidence.
  3. RefineWe shape the chosen direction together through focused rounds of feedback.
  4. DeliverFinal files, a short style guide, and help getting everything launched.
