    A note from Amanda

    Why this matters to me

    I believe what the Bible says, that God's people are destroyed for a lack of knowledge.

    “My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.”

    Hosea 4:6 · NASB 1995

    When I had a lack of knowledge, it almost destroyed my health — and I could have gone my whole life that way. But by constantly educating myself, learning, and coming across the right women online who care about educating others, I was finally able to take charge of my own health again.

    I want to help other women see that what society tells them — that being miserable is a normal state of being — is actually not normal, and that they are capable of changing it.
