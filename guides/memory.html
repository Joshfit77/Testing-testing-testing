Natural Support for Memory & Brain Health

A healthy brain thrives on sleep, exercise, social connection and learning. Bacopa improved memory after 12 weeks in several trials, and the aroma of rosemary has been linked with better alertness. Berries rich in anthocyanins, like blueberries, support memory as we age.

Start with the basics

  • Exercise regularly — it grows new brain connections.
  • Keep learning: read, memorize scripture, learn a skill.
  • Stay connected with friends and family.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours — the brain clears waste during sleep.
  • Protect your hearing and control blood pressure.

Foods that may help

  • Eggs
  • Salmon
  • Sardines
  • Walnuts
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Herbs that may help

  • Bacopa — Memory and learning: Bacosides support communication between nerve cells. A review of trials found bacopa improved memory recall and attention after about 12 weeks of daily use.
  • Ginkgo — Circulation: Ginkgolides improve blood flow and make platelets less sticky. Studies found ginkgo modestly increased pain-free walking distance in people with poor leg circulation.
  • Rosemary — Alertness and memory: 1,8-cineole from rosemary's aroma is absorbed into the bloodstream and may influence brain chemistry. Small studies linked breathing in rosemary aroma with better performance on memory and alertness tasks.
  • Sage — Hot flushes and sweating: In small studies, a fresh sage extract reduced the number and intensity of menopausal hot flushes over eight weeks. Sage has long been used for excessive sweating in general.
  • Gotu Kola — Skin repair: Asiaticoside and madecassoside stimulate collagen production. Gotu kola is used in creams for scars, stretch marks and sensitive skin.
  • Green Tea — Calm focus: Green tea's caffeine combined with L-theanine improves attention and alertness with fewer jitters than coffee, according to multiple studies.

Fruits that help

  • Blueberry
  • Honeyberry (Haskap)
  • Pomegranate
  • Avocado

Safety first

Ginkgo increases bleeding risk, especially with blood thinners or before surgery. Bacopa can upset the stomach — take it with food.

When to see a doctor

  • Memory problems that affect daily life or that others notice
  • Getting lost in familiar places
  • Sudden confusion, slurred speech or face drooping — call emergency services (possible stroke)
