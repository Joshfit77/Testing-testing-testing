Medical Disclaimer

Please read this before using any herb, supplement or stack described on this website.

Last updated: October 2026. This page is a general template — have it reviewed for your own situation and location.

The information on this website is for education only. It is not medical advice, and it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Talk to a professional first

Always consult your doctor, pharmacist or a qualified healthcare professional before using herbs, supplements or making changes to your diet — and never stop or change a prescribed medicine without your doctor's advice.

Doses

Doses on this site are typical adult amounts drawn from traditional herbal references and published studies. They are for adults weighing about 100 lb (45 kg) or more, are not scaled by body weight, and are not suitable for children unless a professional advises otherwise. Products vary in strength; always follow the label and your healthcare provider's guidance.

Interactions and side effects

Herbs and some fruits can interact with medicines and health conditions. Our interaction checker lists common interactions but cannot cover every medicine, dose or individual situation. Stop using any herb and seek medical help if you notice side effects or an allergic reaction.

Special situations

Take extra care if you are pregnant, trying to conceive, breastfeeding, elderly, preparing for surgery, or living with a long-term condition such as heart, liver or kidney disease.

Emergencies

If you think you or someone else has taken too much of an herb or is having a serious reaction, call your local emergency number or Poison Control (in the U.S., 1-800-222-1222) right away.

No endorsement

Statements on this website have not been evaluated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Mention of a product or brand does not mean it is endorsed.
