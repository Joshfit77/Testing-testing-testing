Herbs & Supplements Before Surgery

Some herbs thin the blood, change blood sugar or interact with anesthesia. Taking them right before an operation can cause extra bleeding or complications. Planning ahead keeps your surgery safe.

Before your operation

  • Tell your surgeon and anesthesiologist about every herb, vitamin and supplement you take.
  • Stop herbal supplements at least 2 weeks before surgery, or as your surgeon advises.
  • Food amounts of herbs and spices are fine.
  • Don't stop prescribed medicines unless your doctor tells you to.

After your operation

  • Ask your surgeon when it's safe to restart supplements.
  • Topical arnica may help bruising — only on unbroken, healed skin.
  • Pineapple, berries and protein-rich foods support healing.
  • Ginger tea may ease post-operative nausea once your doctor allows.

What could happen & what to do

Herbs that thin the blood, change blood sugar or interact with anesthesia can cause bleeding or complications during and after surgery.

Stop herbal supplements 2 weeks before surgery (or as your surgeon advises) and tell your surgical team everything you take.

Herbs & fruits to avoid

  • Ginkgo — Increases bleeding.
  • Garlic — Supplements increase bleeding.
  • Asian Ginseng — Affects bleeding and blood sugar.
  • Feverfew — Increases bleeding.
  • Reishi Mushroom — Increases bleeding.
  • Valerian — Interacts with anesthesia — taper off.
  • St. John's Wort — Interacts with anesthesia medicines.

Use with caution

  • Ginger — Supplements may increase bleeding.
  • Turmeric — Supplements may increase bleeding.
  • Fenugreek — May affect bleeding and blood sugar.
  • Black Seed — May slow clotting.
  • Cumin — May affect blood sugar.

Get help right away if…

  • Bleeding that won't stop or a rapidly swelling wound
  • Fever, spreading redness or pus from the wound
  • Chest pain, shortness of breath or calf swelling (possible blood clot)
