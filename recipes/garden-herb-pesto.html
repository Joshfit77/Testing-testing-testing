Garden Herb Pesto

In the Kitchen · 10 minutes · Makes About 1 cup

A bright, garlicky sauce full of fresh herbs and heart-healthy olive oil.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • ½ cup fresh parsley
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • ⅓ cup walnuts or pine nuts
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup (120 ml) extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • To taste salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Crush the garlic and let it rest for 10 minutes (this boosts allicin).
  2. Pulse the basil, parsley, garlic and nuts in a food processor.
  3. Add the Parmesan and lemon juice and pulse again.
  4. With the motor running, drizzle in the olive oil until smooth. Season to taste.

Tips

  • Toss with pasta, spread on toast, or drizzle over roasted vegetables and eggs.

Storage

Keeps in the fridge for 5 days with a thin layer of oil on top, or freeze in ice cube trays.

Safety

Contains nuts and dairy. Basil and parsley are high in vitamin K — keep amounts steady if you take warfarin.
