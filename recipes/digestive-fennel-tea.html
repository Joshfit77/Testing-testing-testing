After-Dinner Fennel Tea

Teas · 10 minutes · Makes 1 mug

A sweet, soothing tea to ease bloating and heaviness after a big meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
  • 1 tsp dried peppermint
  • 2 thin slices fresh ginger
  • 1 cup (240 ml) just-boiled water

Steps

  1. Crush the fennel seeds with the back of a spoon to release their oils.
  2. Add fennel, peppermint and ginger to a mug.
  3. Pour over the just-boiled water, cover and steep for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Strain and sip after meals.

Tips

  • If you have heartburn, leave out the peppermint.

Storage

Keep a dry blend of fennel and peppermint in a jar for up to 6 months.

Safety

Peppermint can worsen reflux. Fennel has mild estrogen-like effects — check with your doctor if you have a hormone-sensitive condition.
