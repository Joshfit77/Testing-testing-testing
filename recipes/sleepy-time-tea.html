Sleepy-Time Herbal Tea

Teas · 10 minutes · Makes 1 mug

A gentle, floral bedtime tea that helps you wind down. Chamomile and lemon balm calm the nerves, and a tiny pinch of lavender adds a relaxing aroma.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp dried chamomile flowers
  • 1 tsp dried lemon balm leaf
  • 1 small pinch dried lavender buds
  • 1 cup (240 ml) just-boiled water
  • 1 tsp (optional) honey

Steps

  1. Put the chamomile, lemon balm and lavender in a tea infuser or teapot.
  2. Pour the just-boiled water over the herbs.
  3. Cover the mug or pot (this keeps the calming oils in) and steep for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Strain, stir in honey if you like, and sip slowly 30–60 minutes before bed.

Tips

  • Too much lavender tastes soapy — a pinch is plenty.
  • Make a jar of the dry blend so it's ready every night.

Storage

Store the dry blend in a sealed jar away from light for up to 6 months.

Safety

Can add to the drowsiness of sleep medicines and alcohol. Avoid if allergic to ragweed or daisies. If pregnant, ask your midwife first.
