Thyme & Honey Cough Syrup

Syrups & Remedies · 20 minutes · Makes About 1 cup

A simple kitchen remedy for coughs and sore throats. Thyme helps loosen mucus and honey coats the throat.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp dried thyme (or a handful of fresh sprigs)
  • 1 cup (240 ml) water
  • ½ cup (120 ml) raw honey
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Steps

  1. Bring the water to a boil, add the thyme, cover and turn off the heat.
  2. Steep for 15 minutes, then strain.
  3. Let the tea cool until warm, then stir in the honey and lemon juice until smooth.
  4. Pour into a clean jar.

Tips

  • Adults: 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon as needed, up to 4 times a day. Children over 1: ½–1 teaspoon.

Storage

Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 weeks.

Safety

Never give honey to babies under 1. See a doctor for a cough lasting over 3 weeks, high fever, chest pain or trouble breathing.
