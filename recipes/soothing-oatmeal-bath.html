Soothing Oatmeal Bath

Skin, Hair & Bath · 5 minutes + soaking · Makes 1 bath

A gentle soak for itchy, dry or sun-irritated skin, using the same colloidal oatmeal recommended for eczema.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp (optional) dried lavender or chamomile
  • 1 clean muslin bag or old sock (optional)

Steps

  1. Blend the oats (and flowers) in a blender until they become a very fine powder.
  2. Test: a spoonful stirred into warm water should turn it milky.
  3. Sprinkle the powder into a warm (not hot) running bath and stir, or tie it in a muslin bag.
  4. Soak for 15–20 minutes, then pat skin dry and moisturize.

Tips

  • The tub can get slippery — be careful getting out.

Storage

Keep the oat powder in a sealed jar for up to 3 months.

Safety

Very safe. For babies and young children, ask your pediatrician and leave out the flowers.
