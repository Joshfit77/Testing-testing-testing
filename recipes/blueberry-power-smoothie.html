Blueberry Power Smoothie

Drinks & Smoothies · 5 minutes · Makes 1 large glass

A creamy purple smoothie packed with anthocyanins, fiber and steady energy.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen blueberries
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 handful spinach
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed or chia seeds
  • 1 cup (240 ml) milk or plain yogurt
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon

Steps

  1. Add everything to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth, adding a splash of water if it's too thick.
  3. Pour and enjoy right away.

Tips

  • Add a scoop of protein powder or Greek yogurt for a filling breakfast.

Storage

Best fresh. Freeze leftovers in popsicle molds.

Safety

Very safe for most people.
