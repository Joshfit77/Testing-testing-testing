Mint, Cucumber & Lemon Water

Drinks & Smoothies · 5 minutes · Makes 1 pitcher

A spa-style water that makes it easy to drink more through the day.

Ingredients

  • ½ cucumber, thinly sliced
  • ½ lemon, sliced
  • A handful fresh mint leaves
  • 8 cups (2 L) cold water

Steps

  1. Gently crush the mint leaves in your hand to release their oils.
  2. Add the mint, cucumber and lemon to a pitcher.
  3. Fill with cold water and chill for at least 1 hour.

Tips

  • Refill the pitcher once with water — the flavor lasts for a second round.

Storage

Best within 24 hours; remove the lemon after 4 hours to avoid bitterness.

Safety

Very safe. Lemon acid can affect tooth enamel — rinse with plain water after.
